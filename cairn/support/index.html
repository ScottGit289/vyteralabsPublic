Questions we are asked most

Why does iOS ask for calendar "Full Access" when Cairn only reads?

Because iOS does not offer a read-only choice. Since iOS 17 an app can hold exactly three calendar states: None, Add Events Only (which grants writing without reading), and Full Access (read and write). There is no fourth option. Reading your meetings requires Full Access, so that is what Cairn has to ask for.

Add Events Only is the worst choice for Cairn: it hands over the one power it never uses and withholds the only one it needs. What stops Cairn writing is the code, not the permission — the app contains no calendar-write call, and does not declare the write-only usage description iOS would require to run one. The privacy policy sets this out in full.

I picked the wrong calendar option. How do I change it?

iOS only asks once. Open Settings → Calendar in Cairn and tap Open iOS Settings, or go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Cairn and choose Full Access.

Where is my data, and how do I move it to a new phone?

On your device, in the app's own sandbox. There is no cloud copy, so moving devices is a deliberate act:

  1. On the old device: Settings → Export a backup. You get a plain JSON file.
  2. Get the file onto the new device however you like — AirDrop, Files, email to yourself.
  3. On the new device: install Cairn, then Restore → Replace.

Your normal encrypted iPhone backup also includes Cairn's data, so restoring a device from backup restores your library with it.

Why is there no sync?

Because sync requires either an account with us or your data passing through iCloud, and the promise on the front page is that neither happens. It is a deliberate trade, not an oversight. Export and restore is the supported way to move a library between devices.

The weekly review said something I did not like.

Change its tone. Tap Aa on the review screen, or open Settings → Weekly review, and choose Supportive, Encouraging, Friendly, Just the facts, or Direct. The figures and findings are identical in all five — only the wording changes.

If a review ever comments on you rather than on the work, that is a bug and we want to know. Please send us the exact wording.

Apple Intelligence features are missing or greyed out.

Three things have to be true, and any one of them can be the cause:

  • The device runs iOS 26 or later and supports Apple Intelligence.
  • Apple Intelligence is switched on in iOS Settings, and the model has finished downloading.
  • Settings → Use Apple Intelligence is on inside Cairn.

Settings → What runs on this device tells you which of these is true right now. If none of them are, nothing is lost: the parser, planner, scorer, review engine and search are Cairn's own code and run on every supported device regardless.

Reminders are not arriving.

Check notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Cairn, then tap Settings → Re-check notification permission in Cairn, which also reschedules everything outstanding. Reminders are scheduled by your device; if a to-do has no reminder time set, none is scheduled.

Plan My Day left out something I wanted in.

By design, the automatic plan does not reach into Upcoming or Someday — dragging Friday's job into Monday would overrule a decision you had already made. Use Pulled in by hand on the Plan screen to add anything from Upcoming, Someday or a shelved project. The plan screen also lists everything it held back and why.

Dark mode is not following my phone.

Settings → Appearance offers System, Light and Dark. System follows the device; the other two override it.

Is my data included in my iCloud backup?

Yes, if you use encrypted iPhone backups — Cairn's database sits in the app's sandbox, which is what a device backup covers. That is Apple's backup of your device, not Cairn syncing anything. Cairn itself never contacts iCloud or anything else.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app, and it is gone — there is nothing anywhere else, because there was never anything anywhere else. Settings → Start fresh does the same without removing the app. Both are irreversible, so export a backup first if there is any doubt.

Reporting a bug

Cairn has no crash reporting and no telemetry, which means we learn about problems only when someone tells us. If you can include the following, we can usually work it out without a second email:

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The app version from Settings → About → Version.
  • Your iOS version and device model.
  • A screenshot, if there is anything to see.

Please do not send us a backup file unless we ask — it contains your entire library, and we would rather not have it.

Accessibility

Cairn supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion and increased contrast throughout. If you hit something that does not work with assistive technology, that is a defect and we treat it as one — tell us and we will fix it.
