What to include

SprintLab collects no diagnostics and sends us nothing, so a report is only as good as what you tell us:

  • Your device model and iOS version — Settings → General → About.
  • The SprintLab version, from the app's own Settings screen.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it is something you can see.
Please don't send us your backup export unless we ask.

It contains your whole training history. If a problem genuinely needs it we will say so, and explain what we will do with it and when we will delete it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a wearable or an account?

No. SprintLab works fully with manual entry and needs no account, no sign-up and no internet connection. Every readiness metric can be typed in by hand. A wearable is entirely optional and just auto-fills some numbers.

How does the daily readiness score work?

Each day SprintLab blends your recovery signals — sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, body temperature, stress — with your own check-in on energy, motivation and leg freshness, and your recent training load, into a 0–100 score and a clear call: Sprint, Gym, Recovery or Rest.

Tap any metric on the Readiness screen to enter or edit it. The score updates instantly.

How do I connect Oura?

Go to Settings → Wearables, turn on “Enable wearable sync”, then tap Connect Oura Ring and sign in on Oura's official page. SprintLab never sees your Oura password, and your health data comes straight to your phone. You can set how often it auto-syncs, and disconnect at any time.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Go to Settings → Wearables, enable sync, tap Connect Apple Health, and tap Allow on Apple's permission sheet. SprintLab reads sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate and blood oxygen entirely on your device — no account, nothing leaves your phone.

You can review or revoke access any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

What is “on-device intelligence”, and is my data safe?

It is an optional setting — Settings → Intelligence, off by default — that uses Apple Intelligence to write your insights and daily briefing. It runs entirely on your iPhone: nothing is sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising or model training.

It needs a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled. On other devices SprintLab uses its built-in engine instead and works exactly the same. Turn it off at any time.

Can I return a metric to its wearable value after editing it?

Yes. A manual edit always wins, but the original wearable reading is kept underneath. Open the metric on the Readiness screen and tap Use Oura value to restore it.

How do I back up or move my data?

Use Settings → Export backup to save a copy via the system share sheet — Files, AirDrop, email. On another device, use Import backup and choose that file.

Because your data lives only on your device, export a backup periodically. There is no cloud copy, and nobody else holds one for you.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Delete all data, or simply delete the app. Everything is stored on your device, so there is nothing held anywhere else to remove.

Why is a personal best marked “wind-aided”?

For sprints up to 200m, a tailwind over +2.0 m/s makes a time wind-aided — it is kept and shown, but never counted as a legal PB. You can hide wind-aided results entirely in Settings.
