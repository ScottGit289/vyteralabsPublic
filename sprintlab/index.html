01

Performance readiness

A 0–100 readiness score each morning, and a clear call to go with it: Sprint, Gym, Recovery or Rest.

It blends sleep, recovery, HRV, resting heart rate, body temperature and stress with your own check-in on energy, motivation and leg freshness. Every value is editable by hand and labelled by where it came from — Manual, Oura, Calculated or Estimated — so you always know what the number is built on.

“Today matches your previous personal-best days.” “HRV is 12% above your monthly average.” The score is only useful if it tells you why.

02

Analytics that find the pattern for you

SprintLab looks for the relationships that matter without being asked: sleep against 100m time, HRV against top speed, stress against performance, gym load against speed.

Correlations, trends, and the recovery conditions your personal bests actually happen in. It gets sharper the more you log — the analysis grows with your data rather than assuming it on day one.

03

Personal bests, kept honestly

30m to 400m

Every standard distance, plus custom ones for the sessions that don't fit a track.

Timing kept separate

Electronic and hand times never mix. A hand time cannot overwrite an electronic record.

Wind legality

Wind readings with automatic flags. Over +2.0 m/s is marked wind-aided and never becomes a legal PB.

Start type

Block, standing and flying starts tracked separately, because comparing them is meaningless.

PB celebrations tell you exactly which time you beat.

04

Training log and tools

Log a session in seconds: type, timed reps, gym work, notes, injuries, sleep. Progress graphs per distance, improving and declining trends, and a consistency score that rewards showing up week after week.

Sprint calculator

Speeds in m/s, km/h and mph, equal-speed equivalents across distances, and even-pace splits.

Age grading

WMA-style age grading for masters athletes, computed entirely offline.

05

Wearable sync, if you want it

Connect an Oura Ring and sleep, recovery, HRV, resting heart rate, temperature and stress fill themselves in. Sign-in uses the vendor's own secure flow, so SprintLab never sees your password, and your health data comes straight to your phone.

It is entirely optional. The whole app works on manual entry alone, and you can disconnect at any time.

06

Private by design

Your data is stored on your device. There is no account, ever — nothing to sign up to and nothing to sign in to. There are no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data leaves your device only when you press Export backup, which hands a copy to the iOS share sheet for you to send wherever you choose. Full import too, and you can delete everything in two taps.

The one exception, stated plainly: connecting a wearable uses a small sign-in relay so the confidential app secret never ships inside the app. It brokers the sign-in exchange only, keeps nothing, and never receives your health data. The privacy policy covers it in full.

07

Built properly

Dark mode, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type throughout.

Whether you are chasing a school record, a masters title or your first sub-13 100m, SprintLab keeps the numbers straight so you can concentrate on running fast.
