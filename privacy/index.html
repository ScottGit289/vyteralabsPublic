Privacy Policy

Controller: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 4 October 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

This policy covers this website, contacting us, and our Instagram and Facebook accounts, including the tool we use to run them. It is published by Vytera Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 17389107, which is the data controller.

Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked from that app's page. Where an app's policy and this one differ, the app's policy applies to that app.

The short version
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • If you email us, we use your message to reply to you, and for nothing else.
  • On Instagram and Facebook we post to our own accounts and read the public comments and statistics on our own posts. We do not sell or share that information, and we do not build profiles of people.

This website

The site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics.

  • Hosting. As with any website, the company that hosts these pages receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in standard server logs. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.
  • Fonts. The pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when a page loads. Google's own privacy policy covers that request.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation. We keep support and enquiry emails for up to two years, then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Instagram and Facebook

We run an Instagram account (@vyteralabs) and a Facebook Page (Vytera Labs Ltd). Meta operates both platforms, and Meta's privacy policy governs what Meta does with your data there.

We manage those accounts with a private tool built on Meta's developer platform. It is used only by Vytera Labs. It offers no login to the public and nobody else can connect an account to it.

What the tool does

  • Publishes posts to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page.
  • Reads statistics for our own account and posts, such as reach, views and likes. These are totals. They do not identify individuals.
  • Reads comments on our own posts: the public username, the comment text, and its time and like count.
  • Lists our own posts, with their captions and links.

What it does not do

  • It does not read private messages, follower lists, or anyone's profile details beyond the public username on a comment.
  • It does not copy comments into a database. Comments are read when needed and are not kept by the tool.
  • It is not used for advertising audiences, and nothing it reads is sold or shared for marketing.

Why we do this, and on what basis

We read comments and statistics to answer people and to understand how our posts perform. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running and improving our own public accounts.

Who helps us

Two service providers process this information on our behalf: Cloudflare, which hosts the tool, and Anthropic, whose AI assistant we use to help run the accounts. Some of this processing may take place outside the United Kingdom, under those providers' own safeguards. The tool keeps an activity log for 90 days, recording which action ran and whether it worked. That log does not contain comments or usernames.

Deleting your data

  • Comments and likes. You control these. Delete your comment or remove your like on Instagram or Facebook and it is gone from our view as well. We hold no separate copy.
  • Emails. Email info@vyteralabs.com from the address you used and ask us to delete the conversation. We will do so within 30 days and confirm when it is done.
  • Anything else. If you believe we hold information about you, email us and we will tell you what we hold and delete it on request.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to or restrict how we use it. Email info@vyteralabs.com. We answer within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This website and our social media accounts are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

If what we do with data changes, this page changes with it, and the effective date above changes too.
