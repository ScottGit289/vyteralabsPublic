What you get

Live counter

Your estimated remaining time on the Home screen and as a Home/Lock-Screen widget.

Lifespan & healthspan

Estimated lifespan, a likely range, your "longevity age" vs your real age, and disability-free years.

Survival odds

Six scores

Your key factors

What's helping and hurting you, and the highest-leverage things to change.

Scenario modelling

See what happens if you optimise your modifiable risks — or if they slide.

02

Built on evidence, not hype

The model starts from an age- and sex-specific mortality baseline and adjusts it with published, all-cause hazard ratios from large cohort studies and meta-analyses — blood pressure, smoking, diabetes/HbA1c, ApoB/cholesterol, fitness/VO₂max, adiposity, activity, alcohol, kidney disease and more. Correlated inputs are grouped so they aren't double-counted, and consumer-wearable signals like HRV are deliberately kept from dominating. Every component shows its source, study, population, effect size and evidence strength.

03

Private by design

Local-only

Your data stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded to Vytera Labs.

No account, no tracking

No sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Export or import your data as a file whenever you want.

Wearables

Apple Health (read-only) and Oura via secure sign-in. Garmin is planned. The app prefers 30/90/365-day averages for stability.

Important. Longevity Counter is a statistical estimate based on population-level evidence. It cannot predict your exact lifespan, never predicts a date of death, and is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional care. Always consult a qualified clinician about health decisions. See the Medical Disclaimer.
