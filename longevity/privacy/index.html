Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 22 August 2026  ·  support@vyteralabs.com

Vytera Labs builds Longevity Counter to be private by design. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version
  • Longevity Counter is local-only — your health information stays on your device.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your data. There is no account system, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no advertising SDKs.
  • We cannot see your data. There is nothing for us to sell, share, or lose.

What data the app processes

You may enter or import health-related information, including: age, sex, body measurements, blood pressure, resting heart rate, VO₂max, steps, exercise, strength training, cholesterol/ApoB/Lp(a), HbA1c and glucose, sleep, HRV, smoking and alcohol, family history, and medical conditions. All of this is used on your device to calculate your longevity forecast and is stored on your device in the app's private storage.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app requests read-only access to specific metrics (such as VO₂max, resting heart rate, HRV, steps, sleep, body measurements and blood pressure). This data is read into the on-device model.

  • We never write data back to Apple Health.
  • Data obtained from HealthKit is not transmitted off your device and is used only to compute and display your forecast, consistent with Apple's HealthKit requirements.
  • You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Oura (optional)

If you connect Oura, you sign in through Oura's own secure authorisation (OAuth) — you never give the app your Oura password. Oura returns an access token (and a refresh token) that the app stores in your device's Keychain. The app uses it to fetch your Oura sleep/HRV/heart-rate data directly from Oura's servers to your device. That request goes to Oura under your authorisation and is subject to Oura's privacy policy. Vytera Labs does not receive or proxy this data. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes the stored tokens.

Garmin

Garmin integration is not yet available. When added, it will follow the same on-device principles described here, and this policy will be updated.

What we collect: nothing

Vytera Labs does not collect, receive, store, or process your data on any server. Specifically:

  • No accounts / sign-in to Vytera Labs.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services. A diagnostics/crash log is written locally and is only ever shared if you choose to export and send it to support.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. This matches the app's bundled Privacy Manifest.

Data you choose to export or share

The app lets you export your data as a JSON file and import it back. When you use the iOS share sheet to send an export or a diagnostics log, that file goes wherever you direct it (e.g. your own files, email, AirDrop). Once it leaves the app it is under your control and the terms of whatever service you send it through.

Children

Longevity Counter is not directed to children and is intended for adults (18+). Do not use the app to enter data for a child.

Your controls

  • Delete all data: Settings → Delete all data removes your profile and all stored forecasts from the device.
  • Revoke Health access: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.
  • Disconnect Oura: Wearables → Disconnect (removes the Keychain tokens).
  • Uninstalling the app removes its on-device data.

Security

Data is stored in the app's sandboxed container. Oura tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Because nothing is transmitted to Vytera Labs, there is no server-side breach surface for your health data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version on this page.

Contact
