Medical Disclaimer

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 22 August 2026  ·  support@vyteralabs.com

Longevity Counter provides a statistical estimate based on population-level epidemiological evidence. Please read and understand the following.

1. Not a medical device

The App is intended for general wellness and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any disease or condition.

2. Not a prediction of your lifespan

The App cannot predict an individual's exact lifespan and never displays a predicted date of death. Figures such as "estimated lifespan", "likely range", "survival probability", "longevity age", and "healthspan" are population-level probability estimates derived from published research applied to the data you provide. The live counter shows an estimate of remaining time based on your median estimated lifespan; it is a statistical figure that changes as your data changes, not a countdown to death.

3. Not a substitute for professional care

Always seek the advice of a physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or health decision. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of anything you have read or seen in the App.

4. Accuracy and limitations

Estimates are only as reliable as the data entered and the assumptions of the underlying model, which is an evidence-assembled statistical model rather than a single prospectively validated clinical risk score. Individual outcomes vary widely and are influenced by many factors not captured here. "Longevity age" and "healthspan" are communication devices, not validated clinical assays.

5. Emergencies

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your doctor or emergency services immediately.

By using the App you acknowledge that you have read and understood this disclaimer.
