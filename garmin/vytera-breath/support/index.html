Vytera Breath

Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Updated: 18 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Vytera Breath is a structured breathwork trainer: eight protocols guided entirely by vibration, with live heart rate through every phase and targets that follow your own last five sessions. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The user guide covers the controls, every setting with its default, and the problems that actually come up.
  • Vytera Breath is not yet on the Connect IQ Store. If you are running it, you sideloaded it, and some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app, usually within a few working days.

What to include

Four things turn a report we cannot place into one we can fix:

  • Your watch model, and the software version it is running — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only sometimes.
  • How you installed it — from the Connect IQ Store, or sideloaded.

Common questions

The coherence indicator shows --.

Your watch exposes neither a variability figure of its own nor the beat-to-beat intervals needed to compute one. The app says so rather than inventing a number. Heart rate, holds and every protocol still work normally.

The check-in alert fired and I feel fine.

It fires when your heart rate goes below your check-in floor, or when the sensor loses your pulse during a hold — a loose strap will do the second. Tighten the watch a finger’s width above the wrist bone, and raise the floor under Settings → Low HR check-in if your resting rate is genuinely low.

Can I make it hold me longer than three minutes?

No. Whatever you manage, the app will not prescribe a hold past three minutes, and that ceiling is not a setting.

Why is there no units setting?

Nothing here is measured in kilometres, miles, metres or feet — only seconds, beats per minute and milliseconds, none of which differ between metric and imperial. A units toggle would have been a control that changed nothing. There is a hold-time format setting instead.

Does it need my phone?

No. The protocols, the sensors, the history and the progression all run on the watch. Settings can be edited on the watch or from Garmin Connect, whichever you have to hand.

Feature and device requests

Both are welcome at the same address. If you want a watch supported that is not on the list, say which model you have — that is the single most useful thing you can tell us, and the list grows that way.
