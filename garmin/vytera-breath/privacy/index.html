Vytera Breath

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 26 September 2026  ·  support@vyteralabs.com

This policy covers Vytera Breath, a Garmin Connect IQ app published by Vytera Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 17389107.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app has no network access of its own and sends nothing to Vytera Labs. We could not receive your data if we wanted it.
  • What it stores, it stores on your watch, and uninstalling removes it.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no sign-up, no account and no identifier. We operate no server for this app, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind. Vytera Labs receives no data about you or your use of it — not your settings, not your results, not the fact that you opened it.

Permissions, and why

Vytera Breath requests the following, and each one does a single job:

PermissionWhy it is needed
FitRecords the session as a breathwork activity — meditation, with a breathing sub-sport — and adds a lap per round. Without it there is no activity in Garmin Connect at all.
FIT ContributorWrites the developer fields that graph your breathing into that activity. It is a separate right from recording the activity, so it is a separate permission. Without it the activity still appears, with heart rate and laps, but none of the breathing data.
SensorLive heart rate during every phase, and beat-to-beat intervals where your watch exposes them. Without it there is no biofeedback: no heart-rate-guided holds, no recovery reading, no coherence band.
User ProfileRead once, and only to seed a sensible default for the low-heart-rate check-in. Without it that check-in defaults to 40 bpm, which is unremarkable for a trained endurance athlete and alarming for most people.

No location, no communications and no background permission is requested. The app cannot use the network.

What it stores, and where

Your settings, your configured protocols and your session history — the last few holds the targets are built from — live in the watch’s own application storage. Settings → Reset progression clears the history, and uninstalling removes all of it.

What leaves your watch

A saved session is written to your watch as an ordinary activity, which is the point of the app. Your watch then syncs it to Garmin Connect over the same path as every other activity it records — this app takes no part in that and sends nothing itself.

Once the activity reaches Garmin Connect it is held by Garmin under Garmin’s own privacy policy, not ours. We never receive a copy. If you discard a session rather than saving it, nothing is written at all.

Heart-rate data from saved sessions feeds your watch’s own Body Battery and stress metrics exactly as any recorded activity does. This app cannot read those scores and cannot change them.

Garmin

Your watch, Garmin Connect and the Connect IQ Store are Garmin’s, not ours. Anything you do there — installing the app, editing its settings from your phone, or syncing your watch — is governed by Garmin’s own privacy policy. We have no visibility of it and no control over it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app from the watch. Its settings and any state it kept go with it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no request to make of us — we hold nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page changes with it, and the effective date above changes too. We will not quietly start collecting something.
