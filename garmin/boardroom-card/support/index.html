Boardroom Card

Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Updated: 26 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Boardroom Card turns your Garmin watch into a business card: a full-screen QR code that a phone camera saves straight to its contacts, with up to three profiles, LinkedIn-only cards and a Wi-Fi card. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The user guide covers the controls, every setting with its default, what goes into the code, and the problems that actually come up.
  • Boardroom Card is not yet on the Connect IQ Store. If you are running it, you sideloaded it, and some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app, usually within a few working days.

What to include

Four things turn a report we cannot place into one we can fix:

  • Your watch model, and the software version it is running — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • The phone that scanned it, if the problem is a code that will not scan — make and model, and which app you scanned with.
  • How you installed it — from the Connect IQ Store, or sideloaded.

Please do not send us your contact details or a photo of a code: we do not need them to help, and a photo of a code is a copy of everything in it.

Common questions

Does the other person need an app?

No. The code is a standard vCard, which the built-in camera on current iPhones and Android phones reads directly and offers to save as a contact.

Does it need my phone, or a signal?

No. The codes are generated on the watch, and the app has no network access at all. Your phone is only needed to enter or change your details.

Why does it say “Preparing code...” the first time?

The watch draws each code itself. It takes a moment the first time a card is shown; the watch then keeps the finished code, and it appears at once from then on until you change that card's details.

Some of my details are missing from the saved contact.

If a card is over the size a phone can read reliably from a watch, the app trims it — note first, then website, title, company and LinkedIn — and never your name, mobile or email. The preview (press Start) says Shortened to fit when it has. Shorter fields bring the rest back.

Is the Wi-Fi password safe in the code?

It is exactly as safe as showing someone the password: anyone who can scan or photograph your screen can read it. Use a guest network you are happy to share.

My watch is not supported.

Boardroom Card needs Connect IQ API 5.0 or later. Update your watch's software first; if it is still missing, write in with the model.

Feature and device requests

Both are welcome at the same address. If you want a watch supported that is not on the list, say which model you have — that is the single most useful thing you can tell us, and the list grows that way.
