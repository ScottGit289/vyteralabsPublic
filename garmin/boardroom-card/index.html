Your business card
on your wrist.

Open the app and a full-screen QR code appears. The other person points their phone camera at your watch and your contact card opens, ready to save — name, title, company, mobile, email, website, LinkedIn. Nothing to install on their side, and nothing sent from yours.

01

One scan, one contact

The code is a standard vCard, the format every phone's address book already understands. iPhone and Android cameras read it directly and offer to create the contact — no app, no account, no typing a number into someone else's phone while they wait.

Built for the screen it is on

The code is sized to the largest square your screen can show — on a round watch, the square inside the circle, not the one around it — with whole pixels per module and a clean white margin, so it scans first time.

Pure black on pure white

Whatever accent you choose, the code itself stays black on white. Scanners need contrast, and a styled code that does not scan is a business card nobody receives.

Check before you share

Press Start, or tap the screen, and the code turns into a text preview: name, title and company. You see exactly what is about to leave your wrist.

Honest about size

A code has a limit before it gets too dense for a phone to read off a watch. If your details exceed it, the note is shortened first, then the least essential fields go — never your name, mobile or email — and the preview says so.

02

More than one card

Most people are more than one person at work. Keep up to three profiles — Work, Personal and Board, or whatever you call them — and move between them with the down button or a swipe.

Three profiles

Each with its own label, name, title, company, mobile, email, website, LinkedIn and a short note. Switch off the ones you do not need and they disappear from the watch.

LinkedIn cards

Each profile can add a LinkedIn-only card: a much smaller code that scans instantly across a crowded room. Give it its own name so you always know which card you are on.

A Wi-Fi card

Optional: one scan joins a guest to your network, without spelling out a password. Off until you switch it on.

One accent colour

A companion to the Boardroom dress watch face: black, minimal, and a single accent of your choice — gold, silver, rose gold, emerald, sapphire or crimson.

03

Devices and data

Watches: 72 models on Connect IQ API 5.0 or later — among them the fēnix 7, 8 and 9 families, epix (Gen 2) and epix Pro, Enduro 3, the Forerunner 165, 255, 265, 570, 955, 965 and 970 families, Venu 2, 3, 4, Sq 2 and X1, vívoactive 5 and 6, Instinct 3 and Instinct Crossover AMOLED, MARQ (Gen 2) and D2. Round, rectangular and semi-octagonal screens are laid out from the screen at runtime.

Permissions: none. The app requests no sensor, no location, no network and no background access.

Data: no account, no companion app, no server. Your details live in the app's settings on your watch and phone. They leave your wrist in one way only: when you show a code and someone scans it. Nothing is ever sent to Vytera Labs.

The code is generated on the watch itself. The first time each card is shown it takes a moment to prepare; after that the watch keeps it, and it appears at once.
