Boardroom Card

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 26 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

This policy covers Boardroom Card, a Garmin Connect IQ app published by Vytera Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 17389107.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app requests no permissions, has no network access, and sends nothing to Vytera Labs. We could not receive your data if we wanted it.
  • Your details leave your watch in one way only: when you show a code and someone scans it.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no sign-up, no account and no identifier. We operate no server for this app, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind. Vytera Labs receives no data about you or your use of it — not your contact details, not your settings, not the fact that you opened it.

Permissions

Boardroom Card requests no permissions at all: no sensors, no location, no communications, no background access and no activity recording. It cannot use the network.

What you enter

The app is only as useful as the details you give it, and it is worth being clear about what they are. Depending on what you fill in, the app's settings can hold, for each of up to three profiles: a label, your full name, job title, company, mobile number, email address, website, LinkedIn address and a short note — and, if you use the Wi-Fi card, a network name and its password.

What it stores, and where

  • Your settings are stored by the app on your watch. When you edit them in the Connect IQ app on your phone, Garmin's apps and services carry them to the watch — see Garmin.
  • The finished codes. Drawing a code takes the watch a moment, so the app keeps each finished code in its own storage on the watch, together with the details it was drawn from, and redraws it only when those details change.

Nothing is stored anywhere else by us. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the watch.

What leaves your watch

Only what you show. When you display a card, the code on the screen contains that card's details, and anyone who scans it — or photographs your screen — receives them. That is the purpose of the app, and it happens only when you choose to show a card.

Once someone has scanned a card, the copy on their phone is theirs, held under their own phone's and apps' terms. Neither we nor the app can see, change or recall it.

A Wi-Fi card contains your network password in readable form. Show it only to people you would tell the password to, and prefer a guest network.

Garmin

Your watch, Garmin Connect, the Connect IQ app and the Connect IQ Store are Garmin's, not ours. Anything you do there — installing the app, entering its settings on your phone, or syncing your watch — is governed by Garmin's own privacy policy. We have no visibility of it and no control over it.

Deleting your data

Clear any field in the app's settings to remove it from your cards, or uninstall the app from the watch to remove everything it stored. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no request to make of us — we hold nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page changes with it, and the effective date above changes too. We will not quietly start collecting something.
