Boardroom Card

User Guide

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  For: Boardroom Card on 72 Garmin watches  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

A business card on your wrist: a full-screen QR code that a phone camera saves straight to its contacts. Up to three profiles, LinkedIn-only cards and a Wi-Fi card. Everything runs on the watch.

If you read nothing else
  • Fill in your details in the Connect IQ app on your phone: Boardroom Card → Settings, then tap Save. Leaving the page without saving loses your changes.
  • Down for the next card, Start (or tap) to see what you are sharing, Back to leave.
  • Hold the watch 15–25 cm from the other person's phone, screen facing it, and turn the brightness up.
  • Anything in a code — including a Wi-Fi password — can be read by anyone who can see your screen.

1. Setting up

  1. Install Boardroom Card from the Connect IQ Store.
  2. On your phone, open the Connect IQ app (or Garmin Connect → Connect IQ Apps), choose your watch, then Boardroom Card → Settings.
  3. All settings are on one page. Fill in the fields that start Profile 1: — at least Profile 1: Full name.
  4. Tap Save at the top of the page. If you go back without saving, your changes are lost.
  5. Let the watch sync with your phone, then open Boardroom Card on the watch.

Until a profile has a name, the watch shows Add your details in the Connect IQ app on your phone instead of a code. A contact card without a name is not a contact card.

Using version 1.0.0? Its settings were split into sections that the Connect IQ app could not save. Update to 1.0.1 or later from the Connect IQ Store; anything you had already saved is kept.

2. The cards

The app builds a list of cards from your settings and moves through them in order, wrapping round at the end.

LinkedIn cardYour LinkedIn address alone — a much smaller code that scans faster and from further away.For each enabled profile with a LinkedIn address and Extra LinkedIn-only card on.

The label at the top of each screen tells you which card you are on: the profile's label for a contact card, the LinkedIn card's own name (LinkedIn if you leave it empty), or Wi-Fi. The dots at the bottom show where you are in the list.

4. Sharing a card

Turn the watch towards the other person and let them point their phone's camera at it. On an iPhone, the Camera app shows a contact banner — tap it, then Create New Contact. On Android, the Camera or Google Lens offers Add contact.

What helps a first-time scan: a distance of 15–25 cm, the screen square to the phone rather than tilted, and the watch's brightness turned up. Most phones do not need to be close; a very close phone often cannot focus.

The first time each card is shown, the watch spends a moment drawing its code and says Preparing code.... It keeps the finished code, so the next time — and every time after, until you change that card's details — it appears at once.

5. What is in the code

The contact card is a standard vCard 3.0, which every phone's address book reads. Empty fields are left out entirely rather than sent blank. A website or LinkedIn address typed without https:// gets it added, and a bare LinkedIn handle such as jane-smith becomes https://www.linkedin.com/in/jane-smith.

A QR code shown on a watch has a practical size limit: past it, the squares get too small for a phone camera to read at arm's length. On most watches the limit is 331 bytes — comfortably more than a full card, which is usually 250–300. Smaller screens get a smaller limit. If your details go over it, the app trims in this order, and never touches your name, mobile or email:

  1. The note is shortened, then dropped.
  2. The website goes.
  3. The job title goes.
  4. The company goes.
  5. The LinkedIn address goes.

When that happens, the preview screen says Shortened to fit. If even name, mobile and email will not fit, the card says Card too long - shorten fields in settings..

6. Settings

All settings are edited in the Connect IQ app on your phone, on one page, in this order: Profile 1, Profile 2, Profile 3, Wi-Fi card, then Accent colour and Card order. Each title starts with its section, so you always know which profile you are editing. Every text field takes up to 64 characters.

Tap Save when you are done. Leaving the page without saving discards every change since the last save.

7. The glance

On watches with glances, Boardroom Card shows the label of your first enabled profile in your accent colour. Select it to open the app on your first card. Glances near the top or bottom of the list are partly hidden by the curve of a round screen, for every app; in the middle of the list it is shown in full.

8. When something is wrong

The phone will not scan the code.

Move the phone to 15–25 cm and hold it square to the screen, turn the watch's brightness up, and check nothing is reflecting off the glass. If a full contact card is still stubborn, try its LinkedIn card: a much smaller code, which scans from further away.

The watch says “Add your details in the Connect IQ app on your phone”.

No enabled profile has a full name yet. Add one in the phone app's settings, save, and let the watch sync.

My details disappear when I leave the settings page.

Tap Save at the top of the settings page before you go back; going back without saving discards the changes. If there is no Save button, or no single page of settings with titles starting Profile 1:, you have version 1.0.0: update Boardroom Card from the Connect IQ Store. On iPhone, if the Save button vanishes after you change Accent colour or Card order, that is a Connect IQ app issue: save your text fields first, then change those two settings and save again.

I changed my settings but the watch still shows the old card.

Check you tapped Save. Saved settings reach the watch at its next sync with your phone; to force one, open Garmin Connect and sync the watch. Then close and reopen Boardroom Card.

The preview says “Shortened to fit”.

Your details were over the size a phone can read reliably from your watch, so the app trimmed the least essential fields (see section 5). Shorten the note or website and the full card comes back.

I installed it by sideloading and there are no settings on my phone.

Garmin's phone app offers settings only for apps installed from the Connect IQ Store. Install it from the store to edit your details on your phone.
