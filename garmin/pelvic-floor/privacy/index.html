Anchor Pelvic Floor

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 18 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

This policy covers Anchor Pelvic Floor, a Garmin Connect IQ app published by Vytera Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 17389107.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app requests no permissions at all — not network, not position, not sensors. It has no way to send anything anywhere.
  • What it stores, it stores on your watch, and uninstalling removes it.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no sign-up, no account and no identifier. We operate no server for this app, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind. Vytera Labs receives no data about you or your use of it — not your programme, not your sets, not the fact that you opened it.

This matters more here than in most apps. Incontinence after prostate surgery is the symptom men are least willing to discuss, and the record this app keeps is a record of how that is going. It never leaves your wrist.

Permissions, and why

None. The app's manifest requests no permissions whatsoever.

No Communications, so it has no network access and cannot transmit anything. No Positioning, so it does not know where you are. No Sensor or Sensor History, so it reads nothing from your body. No Background service, so it does nothing when it is closed.

The only thing it asks of the watch is to vibrate, which needs no permission.

What it stores, and where

Two things, both in the watch's own application storage and nowhere else:

  • Your settings — stage, hold and rest lengths, repetitions, sets a day, cue style.
  • Your record — the number of sets completed on each of the last 120 days. Days, not times: there is no timestamp of when you did a set, because nothing needs one.

If you installed the app from the Connect IQ Store, your settings are also visible and editable in Garmin Connect on your phone, because that is how Connect IQ settings work. That exchange is between your watch, your phone and Garmin — we are not part of it and see none of it.

What leaves your watch

Nothing is recorded. No activity, no FIT file, no timeline entry, nothing in Garmin Connect's activity feed. A pelvic floor set does not appear anywhere your friends can see it, because the app never writes one.

Health data

The app reads no health or fitness data from your watch. It does not touch heart rate, steps, sleep, stress or anything Garmin records. What it holds is a count of exercise sets you told it about by pressing a button.

Garmin

Your watch, Garmin Connect and the Connect IQ Store are Garmin's, not ours. Anything you do there — installing the app, editing its settings from your phone, or syncing your watch — is governed by Garmin's own privacy policy. We have no visibility of it and no control over it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app from the watch, and the settings and the record go with it. To clear the record while keeping the app, use Erase record in the menu. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no request to make of us — we hold nothing.

Children

The app is intended for adults and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page changes with it, and the effective date above changes too. We will not quietly start collecting something.
