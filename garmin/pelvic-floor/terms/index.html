1. The App

Anchor Pelvic Floor is a timer and counter for pelvic floor muscle exercises, aimed at men recovering from prostate surgery. It times a programme you configure, signals each phase by vibration, and keeps a count of completed sets. It runs on the watch alone: it has no account and no server operated by us, and it does not require the separate Anchor iPhone app, which is a distinct product with its own terms. 3. Not a medical device

Anchor Pelvic Floor is not a medical device. It does not assess, diagnose, screen, monitor, treat or alleviate any disease, injury or disability, and it is not intended to. It times an exercise programme and counts what you tell it you have done.

It cannot tell whether you are exercising the right muscle. That requires a physical examination by a person — usually a men’s health physiotherapist or a continence nurse. Nothing the App shows is confirmation that your technique is correct, and a completed set is a record that a timer ran, not evidence of benefit.

The App gives no medical advice and makes no diagnosis. The starting programmes it offers are conservative general starting points drawn from published UK patient guidance; they are not a prescription, and they are not tailored to you. A programme given to you by a physiotherapist, continence nurse, surgeon or other clinician takes priority over anything in the App.

The App holds the timed programme when you tell it a urinary catheter is still in place, because standard UK guidance after a prostatectomy is to begin active pelvic floor exercises once the catheter has been removed. That hold is a convenience, not clinical advice, and the override exists precisely because your own clinical team may tell you otherwise. Follow them, not the App.

Seek medical attention — not support from us — if leaking worsens, if it has not improved by around three months after surgery, if you experience pain, blood in your urine or a burning sensation, or if you are unable to pass urine at all. Do not delay contacting a clinician because an app appeared to show progress.
