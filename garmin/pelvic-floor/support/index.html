Anchor Pelvic Floor

Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Updated: 18 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Anchor Pelvic Floor times the pelvic floor exercises men are given after prostate surgery, buzzes at every change, and counts the day. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The user guide covers the buttons, every setting with its default, and what the app does while a catheter is still in.
  • Anchor Pelvic Floor is not yet on the Connect IQ Store. If you are running it, you sideloaded it, and some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps. Stage, sets a day and cues are all on the watch for that reason.

If it is not the app

We can help with the software. We cannot help with your recovery, and we will not try. Speak to your GP, your continence nurse or your surgical team if leaking is getting worse, if it has not improved by around three months after surgery, if you have pain or blood in your urine, or if you cannot tell whether you are squeezing the right muscle. If you cannot pass urine at all, treat it as urgent.

A men's health physiotherapist can check your technique with a physical examination. No app can, and this one does not pretend to.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app, usually within a few working days.

What to include

Four things turn a report we cannot place into one we can fix:

  • Your watch model, and the software version it is running — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only sometimes.
  • How you installed it — from the Connect IQ Store, or sideloaded.

Common questions

It says my programme is on hold.

Your stage is set to Catheter still in. UK guidance after a prostatectomy is to begin active pelvic floor exercises once the catheter has been removed. Press the menu button and choose My catheter is out when that happens, or Told to start now if your team has specifically told you to start sooner.

I pressed back by accident and it recorded the set.

That is intended: a set stopped early still counts, because stopping when the muscle gives out is correct technique. On the summary screen, press the menu button and choose Undo it.

The watch does not buzz.

Check the watch's own vibration setting first — the app respects it rather than talking over it. Then check Cues in the menu is set to Vibration only or Tones rather than Silent. A few devices have no vibration motor at all, and there the app falls back to the screen.

I cannot find the settings in Garmin Connect.

Sideloaded apps do not get phone-side settings; only apps installed from the Connect IQ Store do. Stage, sets a day and cues are all reachable from the menu button on the watch, which covers what most people change.

My sets are not showing up in Garmin Connect.

They will not. The app records no activity and writes no FIT file — it is an exercise timer, not a workout recorder, and a pelvic floor set is not something most men want appearing in a shared activity feed. The count lives on the watch.

Can I use the numbers my physiotherapist gave me?

Yes, and you should. Set your stage first, because changing it resets the programme, then edit hold length, rest length, repetitions and sets a day. Theirs beat ours every time.

My watch is not supported.

Tell us the model. The device list is set by the app's manifest and can be widened in a future release.

Feature and device requests

Both welcome, at the same address. Device requests are usually the easiest thing to act on. Requests to add a bladder diary or the reading are more likely to be answered with a link to the iPhone app, which already has both and is a better place for them.

Privacy and terms

The Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use both apply. The short version of the first: the app requests no permissions at all, so it has no way to send anything anywhere, and does not.
