Cortisol Load

Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Updated: 27 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Cortisol Load estimates physiological stress and recovery load from your Garmin watch's own signals, against your personal baseline. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The user guide covers the pages, the first seven days, the HRV check, every setting with its default, and what each status means.
  • Cortisol Load is not yet on the Connect IQ Store. If you are running it, you sideloaded it, and some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps. The settings menu on the watch works either way.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app, usually within a few working days.

We cannot give medical advice. If you are worried about your health, please speak with a healthcare professional rather than us.

What to include

  • Your watch model, and its software version — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Which page, and what the Signals page shows for each row.
  • How many days you have used the app, and your baseline and sensitivity settings.

Common questions

Does it measure my cortisol?

No. No watch can measure cortisol. Cortisol Load estimates how far your heart-rate, stress, recovery and sleep signals are from your own normal. It is named for the link between those signals and the body's stress response, but it does not estimate a cortisol level and is not a medical device.

Why is there no score yet?

The first seven days build your baseline, and the app shows Baseline building rather than a number that would look precise but mean little. Days count only when you open the app, so open it once a day.

Why does Sleep say n/a?

Your watch shows you a sleep score, but Garmin lets apps read it only where the watch’s firmware provides it — Connect IQ API 6.0.2, currently the fēnix 8, Forerunner 570 and 970, Venu 4, vívoactive 6, Enduro 3 and others. Where it is not provided, Sleep shows n/a and the score is built from the other signals. If a firmware update adds it, Cortisol Load starts using it automatically, with no app update needed.

Why not use Garmin's overnight HRV?

Garmin does not make overnight HRV or HRV Status available to Connect IQ apps. The two-minute HRV check in the menu is the only way an app can measure HRV, and it is a different measurement — so it is compared only with your own earlier checks.

My score is high after a hard training block.

That is the expected response: training is a stressor, and a healthy one. The Explanation page will usually name Training load and Resting HR. It is not a recommendation to stop training.

My score has been high for days.

Persistent changes in these signals have many possible causes — sleep, training, hydration, alcohol, illness, travel. The app cannot tell which. If you are concerned about how you feel, speak with a healthcare professional.

It will not install on my watch.

Cortisol Load needs a round-screen watch on Connect IQ API 5.0 or later. The full list is in the user guide.

How do I delete my data?

Press SELECT → Reset stored data, or uninstall the app. Nothing is held anywhere else.
