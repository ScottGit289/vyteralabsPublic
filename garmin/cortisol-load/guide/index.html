Cortisol Load

User Guide

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Updated: 27 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Cortisol Load scores how far your watch's stress and recovery signals are from your own normal, from 0 to 100, where 50 is your baseline. It is an estimate of physiological stress. It does not measure cortisol and is not a medical device.

If you read nothing else
  • Wear the watch day and night, and open the app once each morning. It reads your signals when opened; it has no background service.
  • For the first seven days it shows Baseline building and no score. That is by design.
  • For HRV, press SELECT → Measure HRV each morning, seated and still, at roughly the same time.
  • 50 is your normal. A high score after hard training is expected; it is not advice to stop.

2. The first seven days

A personal baseline needs days of data. From day 1 to day 6 the score page shows Baseline building — Day n of 7 and the signals being collected, but no number, because a precise-looking score on thin data would mislead. From day 7 the score appears with Moderate confidence, rising to Good at 14 days and High at 28.

Your previous seven days of active minutes are read from Garmin's own history on first launch, so the training-load signal can start sooner — but they do not count towards the seven days, which need physiological data.

3. The HRV check

Garmin's overnight HRV and HRV Status are not available to Connect IQ apps. Cortisol Load offers the next best thing: a two-minute seated measurement of RMSSD from your beat-to-beat intervals.

StepWhat to do
1On the score page press SELECT, then choose Measure HRV.
2Sit still, arm resting, and breathe normally for two minutes. The screen counts down and shows the beats collected.
3A clean reading is saved to today and shown in milliseconds. If more than one beat in five had to be rejected as movement or noise, you see Reading unreliable — not saved. Try again, stiller.

Take it at about the same time each morning: a seated spot reading depends on time of day, so each one is compared only with your readings from within three hours of the same time. It is a different measurement from Garmin's overnight HRV, and the two are never mixed.

5. Settings

Set them on the watch: press SELECT → Settings. Or from your phone: Garmin Connect → your device → Connect IQ Apps → Cortisol Load → Settings. Both edit the same values; changes apply when you leave the settings menu.

SettingOptionsDefault
Baseline period7 · 14 · 28 · 90 days28 days
SensitivityLow · Normal · HighNormal
Show contributorsOn · OffOn
Experimental modeOn · OffOff

A shorter baseline reacts faster but is noisier; 90 days is steadier but slow to follow a real change in your fitness. High sensitivity moves the score further for the same change in your signals. Experimental mode adds the respiration spot signal and shows each signal's own 0–100 score on the Signals page. The algorithm's weights and limits are deliberately not settings.

6. How the score works

  • Your baseline for each signal is the median of your recent days, with a robust measure of your normal day-to-day spread. One abnormal day cannot redefine it.
  • Each signal becomes a 0–100 score from how far today is from your baseline, in units of your own spread. 50 is at baseline. The effect of any one signal is capped, so a single extreme reading cannot dominate.
  • Direction matters: lower HRV, lower sleep score and lower Body Battery, and higher resting heart rate, stress and respiration, count towards load.
  • Sleep averages your last three nights, so one short night counts for about a third of a run of short nights.
  • Training load compares your last three days of intensity minutes with your usual level, plus any Garmin recovery time still to run. Unusual load raises it; usual training does not.
  • The composite is a weighted average of the signals present today — HRV 30, resting HR 20, sleep 20, training load 15, stress 10, Body Battery 10, respiration 5 — re-weighted over whichever exist, and at least one must be physiological.
  • Time zones: travelling does not reset your baseline or look like a change in you. Confidence drops one step for that day, because overnight data may be incomplete. If the watch clock jumps backwards, the app shows Clock change detected and no score until the clock is consistent.

7. Confidence

Days of dataConfidence
Under 3Building baseline (no score)
3–6Low (no score shown until day 7)
7–13Moderate

28 or moreHigh

It is lowered further when only one signal is available, when the signals present carry little of the model's weight, or on a day your time zone changed.

8. If something looks wrong

What you seeWhy, and what to do
Sleep shows n/aYour watch has a sleep score, but its firmware does not yet let apps read it. Garmin provides this from Connect IQ API 6.0.2. Until then Sleep is n/a and its weight passes to the other signals. It is picked up automatically if a firmware update adds it.
HRV shows no dataNo check today. Press SELECT → Measure HRV.
Stress or Body Battery shows no dataThe app needs at least eight hours of the watch's own history. Wear it overnight and open the app in the morning.
Still “Baseline building”Days count only when the app is opened. Open it once a day.
High after a hard sessionExpected: training is a stressor. Check whether the contributors are Training load and Resting HR; they usually settle within a day or two.
“Clock change detected”The watch clock moved backwards. It clears once the time is correct again. If it persists, SELECT → Reset stored data starts afresh.

9. Supported watches

51 round-screen watches on Connect IQ API 5.0 or later.

Connect IQ API 6.0 (sleep score readable by apps, per Garmin): D2 Mach 2 Pro · Enduro 3 · fēnix 8 (43 mm, 47 mm, 51 mm), fēnix 8 Pro, fēnix 8 Solar (47 mm, 51 mm), fēnix E · Forerunner 570 (42 mm, 47 mm), Forerunner 970 · tactix 8 · Venu 4 (41 mm, 45 mm) · vívoactive 6

API 5.0 to 5.2: Approach S50, Approach S70 (42 mm, 47 mm) · D2 Air X10, D2 Mach 1, D2 Mach 2 · Descent G2, Descent Mk3 (43 mm, 51 mm) · epix (Gen 2), epix Pro (42 mm, 47 mm, 51 mm) · fēnix 7, 7 Pro, 7X, 7X Pro, and the Solar editions without Wi-Fi · Forerunner 165, 165 Music, 255, 255 Music, 265, 265S, 955, 965 · Instinct 3 AMOLED (45 mm, 50 mm) · MARQ (Gen 2), MARQ Aviator (Gen 2) · Venu 2, Venu 2S, Venu 2 Plus, Venu 3, Venu 3S · vívoactive 5

Not offered: rectangular screens (Venu Sq 2, Venu X1), the Instinct semi-octagon screens and Instinct Crossover, whose hands cross the display, round screens under 260 pixels (Forerunner 255S, fēnix 7S), Edge cycling computers and handheld units, and watches below Connect IQ API 5.0 such as the Forerunner 245 and 945, fēnix 6 and the first Venu.
