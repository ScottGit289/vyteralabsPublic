Cortisol Load

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 27 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

This policy covers Cortisol Load, a Garmin Connect IQ app published by Vytera Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 17389107.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app reads health-related signals from your watch — that is its purpose — but it has no network access and sends nothing to Vytera Labs or anyone else.
  • What it stores stays on your watch for at most 90 days. You can delete it from the menu at any time, and uninstalling removes it.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no sign-up, no account and no identifier. We operate no server for this app, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind. Vytera Labs receives no data about you or your use of it.

What the app reads, on the watch

Cortisol Load reads data your watch already holds, and processes it only on the watch:

  • Garmin's resting heart rate for the day;
  • your all-day stress and Body Battery history for the previous 24 hours;
  • your daily active minutes and Garmin's recovery time;
  • your sleep score, on watches that make it available;
  • your respiration rate, only if you turn on experimental mode;
  • your beat-to-beat heart intervals, only during a two-minute HRV check that you start. The raw intervals are discarded when the check ends; only the result is kept.

These are health-related data. We treat them as such by never letting them leave the device.

Permissions, and why

PermissionWhy it is needed
Sensor HistoryReads your stress and Body Battery history.
SensorReads beat-to-beat intervals during the HRV check, and at no other time.
User ProfileReads Garmin's resting heart rate.

The app has no Communications permission, so it cannot use the network at all, and no Positioning permission, so it never reads your location.

What it stores, and where

One small record per day — the day's signal values, the score and its confidence, and the algorithm version that produced it — kept in the watch's own application storage. Records older than 90 days are deleted automatically. Your settings are stored the same way.

The app writes no activity file and adds nothing to Garmin Connect.

Garmin

Your watch, Garmin Connect and the Connect IQ Store are Garmin's, not ours. The signals the app reads are measured and held by your watch under Garmin's own privacy policy; so is anything you do in Garmin Connect, such as installing the app or changing its settings from your phone. We have no visibility of it and no control over it.

Deleting your data

On the watch, press SELECT → Reset stored data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the app kept. There is nothing held anywhere else, and no request to make of us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page changes with it, and the effective date above changes too. We will not quietly start collecting something.
