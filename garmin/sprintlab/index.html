The session Garmin
cannot time.

SprintLab Track is built for 60m to 400m work — the reps a Run or Track Run profile cannot handle, because auto-lap by GPS is meaningless over 30 to 60 metres and there is no native idea of "rep, rest, set". It times the reps, runs the rest, and saves a normal activity to Garmin Connect.

01

Five screens, one thumb

A sprinter standing on the track in spikes should never have to navigate a menu to begin. The whole app is five screens deep, and the button that moves you forward is always the same one.

Home picks the session and starts it. Ready tells you what is coming. Rep is the elapsed time and deliberately nothing else. Rest is the countdown, what you just ran against your session best, and what is next. Summary is every rep with its drop-off and heart-rate recovery, then save or discard.

A sprinter glancing at the watch mid-effort has perhaps 150 milliseconds of attention, and every extra glyph costs some of it. So the rep screen is one number, filling the display.

02

The session library

Six sessions, chosen with UP and DOWN on the Home screen so the start button is never more than one press away.

6 × 60m off three minutes. 4 × 150m off six. 3 × 300m off ten. 2 × (3 × 200m), four minutes between reps and twelve between sets. A 120–150–180 speed-endurance ladder on walk-back recovery, where you control the rest so the clock counts up rather than down. And a custom session that remembers your own sets, reps, distance and rests.

03

Drop-off, called honestly

Every rep is measured against the best one of that session. When the gap crosses your threshold, the app says so — because a session where quality has gone is a session that should end.

The default thresholds differ by distance for a reason: sprints of 100m and under tolerate around 5% before the quality is gone, while 150–400m efforts are far less forgiving, at about 3%. Both are adjustable between 1% and 25%.

Heart-rate recovery is recorded per rep as the beats dropped between the end of the effort and 60 seconds into the rest. If the watch cannot give a reading, the app records nothing rather than fabricating a number.

04

Devices and data

Watches: 15 models on Connect IQ System 6 (API 4.2) or later — Forerunner 255, 255 Music, 255S, 255S Music, 265, 265S, 955 and 965; fēnix 7, 7S and 7X; epix (Gen 2); Venu 3 and 3S; and Enduro 3.

Permissions: four, each for one job. Fit and FitContributor create the activity and write the per-rep fields into it. Sensor supplies live heart rate on the Ready and Rest screens and the recovery figure. User Profile supplies your zones and resting rate.

Data: no phone, no companion app, no account, no cloud service. Your session goes into the watch's own FIT file and reaches Garmin Connect the same way every other activity on that watch does. Nothing is sent to Vytera Labs.
