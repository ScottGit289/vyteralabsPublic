Box to Box

Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Updated: 26 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Box to Box is a football training recorder for training sessions, small-sided games and 5-a-side. It measures sprints, high-intensity distance, accelerations and decelerations, and saves a native Football activity to the watch. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The user guide covers the controls, every setting with its default, how each metric is measured, and the numbers that most often look wrong.
  • Box to Box is not yet on the Connect IQ Store. If you are running it, you sideloaded it, and some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app, usually within a few working days.

What to include

Four things turn a report we cannot place into one we can fix:

  • Your watch model, and the software version it is running — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only sometimes.
  • Your settings, if the question is about a number — the sprint and high-intensity speeds especially.

Common questions

My distance looks short, or sprints are missing.

Wait for GPS ok before pressing START. Box to Box uses multi-band GPS where your watch has it. Watches receive GPS once a second, so very short bursts are under-counted. Compare sessions with each other on the same watch.

I get 0 sprints.

The default sprint speed is 25 km/h held for one second, which is quick for recreational players. Lower it to 20–22 km/h in Garmin Connect → your device → Connect IQ Apps → Box to Box → Settings.

Can I wear it in a match?

Box to Box is for training. In matches, the referee decides what players may wear, under Law 4 of the Laws of the Game and your competition's rules. The app makes no claim either way.

It will not install on my watch.

Box to Box needs Connect IQ System 7, the level Garmin requires for paid apps. Older models such as the Forerunner 245 and 945, fēnix 6 and the first Venu cannot install it. The full list is in the user guide.

Why does the distance differ from Garmin's own figure?

Garmin filters its distance its own way. Box to Box adds up GPS speed so that high-intensity distance is always part of total distance. Both figures are in the activity, each labelled.

My accelerations are far lower than my club's vest shows.

That is expected. A vest records ten times a second; a watch receives GPS once a second, and a burst shorter than a second cannot be seen at that rate. The definitions are the same; the sampling is not.

Where do my sessions go?

Into your watch’s own activity file, and from there to Garmin Connect the way every other activity on that watch goes. Nothing is sent to Vytera Labs.
