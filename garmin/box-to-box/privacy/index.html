Box to Box

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 26 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

This policy covers Box to Box, a Garmin Connect IQ app published by Vytera Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 17389107.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app has no network access of its own and sends nothing to Vytera Labs. We could not receive your data if we wanted it.
  • What it stores, it stores on your watch, and uninstalling removes it.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no sign-up, no account and no identifier. We operate no server for this app, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind. Vytera Labs receives no data about you or your use of it — not your settings, not your results, not the fact that you opened it.

Permissions, and why

Box to Box requests the following, and each one does a single job:

PermissionWhy it is needed
FitCreates, runs and saves the activity recording, which is what produces the FIT file your watch syncs.
FIT ContributorWrites the football metrics — sprints, high-intensity distance, accelerations and decelerations, per session and per period — into that same file.
PositioningReads GPS speed, from which every football metric is calculated. The route is recorded in the activity file, as it is for any GPS activity.
SensorReads heart rate, from the wrist sensor or a paired chest strap.
User ProfileReads your heart-rate zones, to count the time you spend in each.

Each permission does one job. The app has no Communications permission, so it cannot use the network at all.

What it stores, and where

Your settings, and the summary of your last saved session (so you can view it again from the start screen), live in the watch’s own application storage. The session you record — including its GPS route and heart rate — is written to the watch’s activity file, exactly as a native Football activity would be.

What leaves your watch

This app does record an activity, including your location, and that is the point of it. When you save a session, Box to Box writes an ordinary FIT activity, with its GPS route, to your watch. Your watch then syncs it to Garmin Connect over the same path as every other activity it records — the app takes no part in that and sends nothing itself.

Once the activity reaches Garmin Connect it is held by Garmin under Garmin’s own privacy policy, not ours. We never receive a copy of it. If you discard a session rather than saving it, nothing is written at all.

Garmin

Your watch, Garmin Connect and the Connect IQ Store are Garmin’s, not ours. Anything you do there — installing the app, editing its settings from your phone, or syncing your watch — is governed by Garmin’s own privacy policy. We have no visibility of it and no control over it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app from the watch. Its settings and any state it kept go with it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no request to make of us — we hold nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page changes with it, and the effective date above changes too. We will not quietly start collecting something.
