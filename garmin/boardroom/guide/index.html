Boardroom

User Guide

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd · For: Boardroom on 68 Garmin watches · info@vyteralabs.com

Boardroom is a dress watch face. Out of the box it shows the time and nothing else: thin indices, slim hands and one accent colour. Everything else is yours to switch on.

If you read nothing else
  • Configure it on the watch with Customize, beside Apply, or in Garmin Connect. Both offer the same choices.
  • Every complication is off by default. A bare dial is the face as designed, not a fault.
  • The seconds hand is off by default, and is never drawn with the wrist down.
  • “No events” in the appointment slot is almost always a phone calendar permission, not the face. See section 6.

1. Applying the face

Open the watch face list on your watch, scroll to Boardroom, and choose Apply.

2. Changing what it shows

  • On the watch — select the face and choose Customize, the option beside Apply. Changes apply immediately.
  • From the phone — Garmin Connect → your watch → Connect IQ apps → Boardroom → Settings. Changes reach the watch on its next sync.

Both menus use the same order: how the face looks, then the seconds hand and the always-on display, then the complications. On fēnix 8 watches with Garmin’s own watch face editor, that editor sets only the accent colour; use Customize for everything else.

If you sideloaded the face, use Customize on the watch. Garmin Connect can only edit settings for apps installed from the store.

The accent appears in exactly three places: the twelve o’clock marker, the seconds hand and the centre cap. Choose any of the eight named accents to override the dial’s own. If an accent is too close to the dial to see — Ice on Ivory, say — the face darkens or lightens it just enough to stay visible.

The only other place colour carries meaning: the battery figure turns to the accent colour at 10% or below.

Those are each complication’s preferred places. If one is taken, the next complication moves to the first free place, so all five always fit. A value the watch cannot supply reads -- rather than disappearing.

The time follows your watch’s 12- or 24-hour setting; in 12-hour mode the digital layout shows AM or PM.

6. The next appointment

The appointment comes from the calendar your watch already shows, through Garmin Connect. Boardroom shows the start time of your next event, as NEXT 08:30. If there is nothing coming up, or the watch has no calendar to give it, it shows No events.

If you expect an event and see “No events”:

  • Check the watch’s own Calendar glance. If the glance shows the event and Boardroom does not, write to us. If the glance is empty too, the problem is on the phone.
  • On iPhone, Garmin Connect needs Full Access to calendars: Settings → Apps → Garmin Connect → Calendars. Limited Access leaves the glance working and the complication empty.
  • After changing the permission, sync Garmin Connect and give it a few minutes.

7. With the wrist down

The always-on face is its own design: large digital time, no seconds, no animation, no hands. Or choose Blank to draw nothing at all — the lowest-power choice, and the kindest to an AMOLED panel. Raise your wrist for the full face.

  • AMOLED watches — dimmed white digits on true black, shifted by a few pixels each minute so no pixel holds one value for hours.
  • Memory-in-pixel watches (fēnix 7, fēnix 8 Solar, Forerunner 255 and 955, Enduro 3 and similar) — black digits on white, at the heaviest weight available, because that is what reads best on a reflective screen. These screens rest in this state most of the day, so on them you will see the dial you chose mainly just after a wrist raise. If you would rather not, choose Blank.

The seconds hand, when on, is drawn only while the watch is awake. That is what keeps the always-on display inside its power budget.

8. Watches

Boardroom runs on 68 watches on Connect IQ System 7. Each one was run in the simulator and its screen checked.

Approach S50, S70 42mm and S70 47mm · D2 Air X10, D2 Mach 1, D2 Mach 2 and D2 Mach 2 Pro · Descent G2, Descent Mk3 43mm and Mk3 51mm · Enduro 3 · epix (Gen 2), epix Pro 42mm, 47mm and 51mm · fēnix 7, 7 Pro, 7 Pro (no Wi-Fi), 7S, 7S Pro, 7X, 7X Pro and 7X Pro (no Wi-Fi) · fēnix 8 43mm and 47mm, 8 Pro 47mm, 8 Solar 47mm and 51mm · fēnix E · fēnix 9 43mm and 47mm, 9 Pro 43mm, 47mm and 51mm, 9 Pro Solar 47mm and 51mm · Forerunner 70, 165, 165 Music, 170, 170 Music, 255, 255 Music, 255S, 255S Music, 265, 265S, 570 42mm, 570 47mm, 955, 965 and 970 · Instinct 3 AMOLED 45mm and 50mm · MARQ (Gen 2) and MARQ Aviator (Gen 2) · Venu 2, 2 Plus, 2S, 3, 3S, 4 41mm, 4 45mm, Sq 2, Sq 2 Music and X1 · vívoactive 5 and 6.

Left out after testing: the Instinct Crossover AMOLED, whose physical hands would sit over Boardroom’s own; and the two-colour Instinct 3 Solar and Instinct E, where the grey hour markers vanish and the sub-window cuts into the dial. Watches older than Connect IQ System 7 — including fēnix 6, Forerunner 245 and 945, Venu, Venu Sq and vívoactive 4 — cannot run it.

9. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
The dial shows only the timeThat is the default. Switch on the complications you want.
There is no seconds handIt is off by default. Turn on Seconds hand. It is never shown with the wrist down.
The face turns white when I lower my wristMemory-in-pixel watches show black on white when idle. Set Always-on display to Blank if you prefer.
The appointment says No eventsSee section 6: almost always the phone’s calendar permission for Garmin Connect.
Garmin Connect will not open the settingsThe face was sideloaded. Use Customize on the watch.
Battery drainAlways-on display is the main cost. Set it to Blank and see whether it changes.
A complication shows --The watch has no value for it at the moment, such as heart rate with the watch off the wrist.

Anything else: see Support, or write to info@vyteralabs.com with your watch model and software version.
