01

Two layouts

Analogue

Slim hands and baton indices.

Minimal digital

A single large clock, set in the largest type your screen can hold.

02

Three dials, nine accents

Graphite, Navy and Ivory, each with its own accent — or choose one of eight: Gold, Oxblood, Steel blue, Emerald, Copper, Ice, Platinum and Amber.

The accent appears in exactly three places, so it reads as a detail rather than a decoration.

03

Five optional complications

Date, battery, next calendar appointment, heart rate and steps. Every one is off by default, so the face you see first is the face as designed. Switch on only what you want.

The next appointment comes from the calendar your watch already shows, through Garmin Connect. If your phone has not given Garmin Connect calendar access, the slot reads No events.

04

A quiet always-on display

On AMOLED watches the sleeping face is its own design: large, high-contrast digits, no seconds, no animation, no hands. Easy to read without raising your wrist, and gentle on the battery and the panel.

Settings: in Garmin Connect on your phone, go to the watch, then Connect IQ apps → Boardroom → Settings, and choose the layout, dial, accent and complications. Changes reach the watch on its next sync. On watches that support it, the same choices are under Customize, beside Apply, on the watch itself.

05

Devices and data

Watches: 77 models on the Connect IQ Store listing, from the fēnix 7, epix (Gen 2), Forerunner 255 and Venu 2 generations onwards — including fēnix 8 and E, Forerunner 165, 265, 570, 965 and 970, Venu 3, 4 and X1, vívoactive 5 and 6, Instinct 3 AMOLED, Enduro, MARQ (Gen 2), tactix, Descent, D2 and Approach. The store listing shows whether yours is included.

Permissions: one. Complication Subscriber — Lets the face read the next appointment from the calendar your watch already shows, through Garmin’s own complications interface. It is only used if you switch the appointment complication on, and nothing is read from your phone directly.

Data: the face reads what your watch already knows and displays it. There is no network code, no account, and nothing is sent anywhere. Your settings live in the watch’s own storage.

Price: one payment, $1.99 in the US, or the price shown on the store in your country. No subscription, no trial, no in-app purchase. Garmin’s 48-hour refund window applies.
