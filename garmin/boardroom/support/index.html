Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd · Updated: 5 November 2026 · info@vyteralabs.com

Boardroom is a dress watch face: thin index markers, one accent colour, analogue or minimal digital, and no fitness data on the dial unless you ask for it. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The user guide covers every setting with its default, the watches it runs on, and the problems that actually come up.
  • Boardroom is on the Connect IQ Store. If you sideloaded it instead, some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps. Install it from the store to get them.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the watch face, usually within a few working days.

What to include

Four things turn a report we cannot place into one we can fix:

  • Your watch model, and the software version it is running — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only sometimes.
  • How you installed it — from the Connect IQ Store, or sideloaded.

Common questions

Garmin Connect will not open the settings.

Garmin Connect can only edit settings for apps installed from the store. Use Customize on the watch, beside Apply, which offers the same choices and works however the face was installed. Settings changed in Garmin Connect reach the watch on its next sync.

The appointment slot reads “No events”.

The appointment comes from the calendar your watch already shows, through Garmin Connect. If your phone has not given Garmin Connect calendar access, or there is nothing coming up, the slot reads No events. Check the calendar permission for Garmin Connect in your phone’s settings.

The dial shows nothing but the time.

That is the face as designed. Every complication is off by default; switch on the ones you want in the settings.

The always-on face looks different.

With the wrist down the face switches to large digital time with no seconds, animation or hands, to stay readable and to spare the battery. On AMOLED watches that is dimmed white on black, shifted a few pixels each minute. On memory-in-pixel watches — whose screen stays on all day — it is black on white, so the dial you chose shows only after a wrist raise. Set Always-on display to Blank if you would rather see nothing.

Feature and device requests

Both are welcome at the same address. If you want a watch supported that is not on the list, say which model you have — that is the single most useful thing you can tell us, and the list grows that way.
