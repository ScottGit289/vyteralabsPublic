Vytera Compass

User Guide

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  For: Vytera Compass on Garmin watches and Edge units  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Vytera Compass gives you a dial you can navigate by, with the wind and the sun drawn on it, the weather your watch already holds, and local or UTC time one press apart.

If you read nothing else
  • Magnetic north is always available and always correct. For true north, enter the declination printed on your map.
  • Position features are off by default. The compass, the clock and the weather all work without GPS.
  • Leave with BACK — that is what guarantees the sensors are released.
  • Nothing is recorded: no activity, no FIT file, no track, no timeline entry.

1. The four screens

Press UP or DOWN — or swipe, on a touchscreen — to page between them.

ScreenWhat it shows
CompassDial, needle, three-digit heading, the MAG or TRUE tag, wind arrow, sun marker and bearing-lock chevron. Time and temperature beneath, and your coordinates when a position is held.
WeatherCondition, temperature and feels-like, wind with direction, humidity, precipitation, pressure and trend, three forecast hours, and a dated source line.
Time and sunClock, date, time zone, sunrise, sunset, twilight, the countdown to the next one, and the sun's current bearing and elevation.
SettingsOpened with MENU from anywhere.

2. The buttons

ButtonAction
UP / DOWN, or swipePage between screens.
ENTER (compass screen)Lock the bearing you are facing. Press again while locked to flip to the back bearing.
ENTER (time screen)Switch between local time and UTC.
Long-press ENTERClear the locked bearing.
MENUOpen settings.
BACKExit. Leave this way — it is what guarantees the sensors are released.

3. Walking a locked bearing

StepWhat to do
1Face the way you intend to travel and press ENTER. A chevron appears on the dial.
2Walk. The chevron shows which way you have drifted and by how much.
3Steer until the watch buzzes — that is you back within three degrees of the bearing.
4To come back, press ENTER again. The lock flips to the back bearing, 180° round.
5Long-press ENTER to clear it.

5. If the heading will not settle

Calibrate the watch's magnetometer the way Garmin documents for your model, and keep clear of anything ferrous — a car bonnet, a bike frame, a handlebar mount. If it still refuses, page to Time and sun: the sun's bearing is computed from time and position, not from the magnetometer, and will orient you regardless.

A needle that is steady but jumpy in small steps is smoothing set too low; a needle that lags behind you as you turn is smoothing set too high.

6. If the weather looks stale

It probably is, and the app will have said so. The weather comes from the cache your watch already holds from its last phone sync — the app has no network of its own. Sync the watch with your phone and it refreshes. The age shown beside the reading is the truth about how old it is.

7. On an Edge unit

Several Edge computers have no magnetometer at all. There the app falls back to course-over-ground and the sun's bearing rather than pretending to a heading it cannot measure — which means it needs you to be moving, or to have a position fix, to give you a direction.
