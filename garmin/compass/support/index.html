Vytera Compass

Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Updated: 18 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Vytera Compass is a compass with a bearing lock, the wind and the sun drawn on the dial, the weather your watch already holds, and local or UTC time. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The user guide covers the controls, every setting with its default, and the problems that actually come up.
  • Vytera Compass is not yet on the Connect IQ Store. If you are running it, you sideloaded it, and some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app, usually within a few working days.

What to include

Four things turn a report we cannot place into one we can fix:

  • Your watch model, and the software version it is running — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only sometimes.
  • How you installed it — from the Connect IQ Store, or sideloaded.

Common questions

The heading will not settle, or looks wrong.

Calibrate the magnetometer the way Garmin documents for your watch, and get clear of anything ferrous. If it still refuses, the Time and sun screen gives the sun’s bearing, which is computed from time and position rather than measured, and will orient you regardless.

It says MAG, not TRUE.

That is the app being honest: it shows magnetic north unless a declination is available. Enter the declination printed on your map under Manual declination and it will apply it. See Every setting.

The weather is out of date.

It will say so. The app has no network of its own and reads the cache your watch holds from its last phone sync. Sync the watch and it refreshes.

There is no heading at all on my Edge.

Several Edge computers have no magnetometer. There the app falls back to course-over-ground and the sun’s bearing, which need you to be moving or to have a position fix.

Feature and device requests

Both are welcome at the same address. If you want a watch supported that is not on the list, say which model you have — that is the single most useful thing you can tell us, and the list grows that way.
