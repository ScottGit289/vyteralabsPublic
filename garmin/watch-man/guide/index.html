Watch-Man

User Guide

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  For: Watch-Man on 19 Garmin watches  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Clear the board of pellets without being caught. Four ghosts hunt you, each by its own rule, and the four power pellets are the only thing that turns the chase around.

If you read nothing else
  • Swipe to turn. A turn asked for slightly before a junction is held and taken at the corner, so you do not have to time it precisely.
  • A tap never pauses during play — pausing is on START and BACK only, and BACK needs two presses to leave.
  • The side tunnels slow ghosts to a third speed. They are the escape route.
  • Eat all four ghosts in one frightened window and that window alone is worth 3,000.

1. The controls

InputAction
Swipe up, down, left or rightTurn. This is the primary control.
UP / DOWN buttonsRotate your heading anticlockwise or clockwise.
STARTPause, resume, or restart after game over.
BACKPause. Press again to exit.
TapResume when paused, restart when the game is over.

A turn requested slightly before a junction is held and taken at the corner, so you do not have to time your swipes to the frame.

A tap never pauses during play. On a touch watch a swipe is sometimes reported as a tap, and losing a chase to that would be maddening — so pausing is on START and BACK only. BACK also takes two presses to leave, so a stray press cannot end a good run.

2. The board

A 19 × 19 grid with void corners, so the maze fills a round screen as a disc. There are 150 pellets, which is about seventy seconds of play, and four power pellets at the widest points of the board.

The two side tunnels wrap: leave by one edge and you arrive at the other. The ghost pen sits sealed at the centre.

Everything is drawn from your watch's screen size at runtime — tile size, sprite radii and the HUD — so the board is proportioned for the display you actually own.

3. How the ghosts hunt

Each ghost has its own target, and knowing which is which is most of the skill.

GhostWhat it aims at
RedYour tile, directly. It is the one on your shoulder.
PinkFour tiles ahead of you — it is trying to cut you off, not follow you.
CyanThe red ghost's position reflected through the tile two ahead of you. It gets more dangerous the closer the red one is.
OrangeYou, until it is within eight tiles — then it breaks for its own corner.

They alternate between scatter and chase on a fixed schedule, and reverse on the spot whenever the mode flips. That reversal is your cue that the hunt has just changed.

4. Power pellets and the frightened window

Eating a power pellet frightens every ghost for a limited window. While frightened they can be eaten, and the score doubles each time within that one window: 200, 400, 800, 1600. Clearing all four in a single window is worth 3,000 on its own.

The window does not last for ever, and it gets shorter as you go — see section 6.

6. How it gets harder

What changesHow
Ghost speed0.67× your speed on level 1, rising to 0.92× by level 6.
Tunnel speedGhosts drag to a third of their speed through the side tunnels, at every level.
Frightened windowStarts at seven seconds and shrinks to a floor of two, where it stays.

That floor is a deliberate departure from the arcade. The original game eventually withdraws the power pellet altogether — fair on a 28 × 31 board with long escape runs, but on a 19 × 19 one it removes your only tool and the game becomes unwinnable. Two seconds is short. It is not nothing.

7. Three things worth knowing

The tunnels are the escape route. Ghosts move at a third speed through them at every level, so a tunnel buys you distance that the open board will not.

Bait the orange one. It gives up inside eight tiles and heads for its corner, which makes its quarter of the board the safest place to clear pellets — as long as the others are elsewhere.

Watch for the reversal. When every ghost turns round at once, the schedule has flipped between scatter and chase. If they have just turned towards you, the chase phase has started.

When something is wrong

If a swipe does not register, use the UP and DOWN buttons instead — they rotate your heading and work identically on watches whose touchscreen is disabled or gloved.

Anything else: info@vyteralabs.com, with your watch model and the software version it is running, from Settings → System → About.
