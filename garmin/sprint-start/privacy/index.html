Sprint Start

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 18 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

This policy covers Sprint Start, a Garmin Connect IQ app published by Vytera Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 17389107.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app has no network access of its own and sends nothing to Vytera Labs. We could not receive your data if we wanted it.
  • What it stores, it stores on your watch, and uninstalling removes it.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no sign-up, no account and no identifier. We operate no server for this app, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind. Vytera Labs receives no data about you or your use of it — not your settings, not your results, not the fact that you opened it.

Permissions, and why

Sprint Start requests no permissions at all. Tones and vibration need none, and it asks for nothing else. It has no access to your position, your activity history, your heart rate or your profile.

What it stores, and where

The only thing the app stores is your own settings — the hold ranges, and whether beeps and vibration are on. They live in the watch’s own application storage and are removed when you uninstall the app.

What leaves your watch

Nothing is recorded. Sprint Start never starts an activity, writes no FIT file, and creates no entry in Garmin Connect. A start time exists on the screen until you reset it, and nowhere else.

Garmin

Your watch, Garmin Connect and the Connect IQ Store are Garmin’s, not ours. Anything you do there — installing the app, editing its settings from your phone, or syncing your watch — is governed by Garmin’s own privacy policy. We have no visibility of it and no control over it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app from the watch. Its settings and any state it kept go with it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no request to make of us — we hold nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page changes with it, and the effective date above changes too. We will not quietly start collecting something.
