Vytera Maze

Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Updated: 18 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Vytera Maze is an arcade maze chase built for a round screen: a 19 × 19 board with void corners, four ghosts that each hunt by a different rule, and tunnels that wrap. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The user guide covers the controls, every setting with its default, and the problems that actually come up.
  • Vytera Maze is not yet on the Connect IQ Store. If you are running it, you sideloaded it, and some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the game, usually within a few working days.

What to include

Four things turn a report we cannot place into one we can fix:

  • Your watch model, and the software version it is running — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only sometimes.
  • How you installed it — from the Connect IQ Store, or sideloaded.

Common questions

A swipe did not register.

Use the UP and DOWN buttons instead — they rotate your heading and work identically, including on watches whose touchscreen is disabled or when you are wearing gloves.

Tapping the screen does not pause the game.

That is deliberate. On a touch watch a swipe is sometimes reported as a tap, and losing a chase to that would be maddening, so pausing is on START and BACK only.

How do I get out?

BACK pauses; press it again to leave. It takes two presses so a stray one cannot end a good run.

The ghosts all turned round at once.

The schedule flipped between scatter and chase. They reverse on the spot whenever it does — it is your cue that the hunt has changed.

Feature and device requests

Both are welcome at the same address. If you want a watch supported that is not on the list, say which model you have — that is the single most useful thing you can tell us, and the list grows that way.
