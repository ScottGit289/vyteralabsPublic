Aircraft Dial

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 18 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

This policy covers Aircraft Dial, a Garmin Connect IQ watch face published by Vytera Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 17389107.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The watch face has no network access of its own and sends nothing to Vytera Labs. We could not receive your data if we wanted it.
  • What it stores, it stores on your watch, and uninstalling removes it.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no sign-up, no account and no identifier. We operate no server for this watch face, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind. Vytera Labs receives no data about you or your use of it — not your settings, not your results, not the fact that you opened it.

Permissions, and why

Aircraft Dial requests the following, and each one does a single job:

PermissionWhy it is needed
Sensor HistoryReads the watch’s own recorded history to fill the optional altitude, barometric pressure, temperature, Body Battery and Stress fields. It is read on the watch, for display on the watch.
User ProfileReads your resting heart rate and heart-rate zone ceiling, so the heart-rate sub-dial has a real range to scale its needle against. Without it, that gauge would need an invented range.

Steps, calories, distance, floors and the rest come from the always-available activity interfaces and need no permission.

What it stores, and where

The only thing the app stores is your own settings — theme, second-hand mode, which field sits in each sub-dial, units and the pressure unit. They live in the watch’s own application storage and are removed when you uninstall the face.

What leaves your watch

Nothing is recorded. The face reads what your watch already knows and draws it. It writes no activity, no FIT file and no timeline entry.

Garmin

Your watch, Garmin Connect and the Connect IQ Store are Garmin’s, not ours. Anything you do there — installing the watch face, editing its settings from your phone, or syncing your watch — is governed by Garmin’s own privacy policy. We have no visibility of it and no control over it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the watch face from the watch. Its settings and any state it kept go with it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no request to make of us — we hold nothing.

Children

The watch face is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the watch face does with data, this page changes with it, and the effective date above changes too. We will not quietly start collecting something.
