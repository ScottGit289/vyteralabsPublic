ClearSight

Support

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd · Updated: 5 November 2026 · info@vyteralabs.com

ClearSight is a watch face built to be vibrant and data rich and still readable by someone whose sight is no longer sharp. It offers three layouts and four high-contrast palettes, and sizes the clock to the largest type the screen can hold. Everything it does, it does on the watch.

Before you write in
  • The overview covers the layouts, the palettes and every option.
  • ClearSight is on the Connect IQ Store. If you sideloaded it instead, some Garmin Connect features — phone-side settings among them — are not available to sideloaded apps. Install it from the store to get them.

Getting help

Write to info@vyteralabs.com. It is a small studio, not a support desk: you will get a reply from the person who wrote the watch face, usually within a few working days.

What to include

Four things turn a report we cannot place into one we can fix:

  • Your watch model, and the software version it is running — on the watch, Settings → System → About.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only sometimes.
  • How you installed it — from the Connect IQ Store, or sideloaded.

Common questions

Customize is missing, or Garmin Connect will not open the settings.

If you sideloaded the face, Garmin Connect cannot edit its settings — it can only do that for apps installed from the store. Use Customize on the watch, beside Apply. Older watches with no on-device settings support show only Apply.

A field shows --.

That field’s sensor is not present on your watch, or the watch has no recorded history for it yet. The face shows a dash rather than inventing a number.

The clock is smaller than I want.

Switch the layout to Balanced or Giant clock, or turn the progress ring off. Each frees space, and the clock takes whatever is freed.

Which palette should I use?

Max Contrast for advanced low vision or any colour blindness. Daylight Bold if light text on a dark background looks smeared. Amber Night at night. Neon Night otherwise.

Feature and device requests

Both are welcome at the same address. If you want a watch supported that is not on the list, say which model you have — that is the single most useful thing you can tell us, and the list grows that way.
