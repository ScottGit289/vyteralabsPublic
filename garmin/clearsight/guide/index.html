ClearSight

User Guide

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd · For: ClearSight on 69 Garmin watches · info@vyteralabs.com

ClearSight is a watch face built to stay readable when your sight is no longer sharp, without giving up the data. It measures everything else first and gives the clock whatever space is left, so fewer fields always means a bigger clock.

If you read nothing else
  • Configure it on the watch with Customize, beside Apply. It works whether or not the face came from the store.
  • Want a bigger clock? Choose Balanced or Giant clock, or turn the outer ring off. Each frees space, and the clock takes it.
  • Max Contrast is the clearest palette, and the one to choose for any form of colour blindness.
  • A field whose sensor your watch does not have reads -- rather than an invented number.

1. Applying the face

Open the watch face list on your watch, scroll to ClearSight, and choose Apply. Or install it from Garmin Connect on your phone and it will be offered on the watch after the next sync.

2. Changing what it shows

There are two ways in, and both edit the same settings:

  • On the watch — select the face and choose Customize, the option beside Apply. Changes apply immediately.
  • From the phone — Garmin Connect → Connect IQ → ClearSight → Settings. Changes reach the watch on its next sync.
If you sideloaded the face, use Customize on the watch. Garmin Connect can only edit settings for apps installed from the store, so a sideloaded face appears in the app count but cannot be opened from the phone.

Field labels are off by default. Each field already has an icon and a colour; a caption small enough to fit under a value is too small to be worth reading. Turn them on if you prefer words.

4. The data fields

Any of the five slots can show any of these: Heart rate, Steps, Battery percent, Battery days left, Calories, Distance, Floors climbed, Active minutes today, Active minutes this week, Body Battery, Stress, Temperature, Notifications, Step goal percent. Or Off, to leave the slot empty.

  • Every field is labelled twice, by a solid icon and by a colour, so nothing depends on telling two colours apart.
  • Values never jump in size. Each field is sized for the widest value it can ever show, so steps do not shrink when they pass ten thousand.
  • Numbers over four digits are grouped: 8,432, not 8432.
  • Distance and temperature follow your watch’s units — kilometres or miles, Celsius or Fahrenheit — as set in the watch’s own system settings.
  • Body Battery, stress and temperature come from the watch’s recorded history. Where a watch does not keep that history, the field reads --.

5. The three layouts

LayoutFields shownUse it when
RichAll five: upper pair, lower pair and bottomYou want the most information at a glance. The default.
BalancedThe lower pair and the bottom fieldYou want a clearly bigger clock and can live with three fields.
Giant clockThe bottom field only, if setThe time matters most. Set the bottom field to Off for the clock and date alone.

The clock is sized last. ClearSight lays out the date, the fields and the seconds, then picks the largest of the watch’s own number fonts that fits the space left — measured at the height the digits will actually sit, because a round screen is narrower near the top and bottom. Turning the outer ring off frees its band, and the clock grows again.

Seconds and AM/PM sit beside the clock, or on a line beneath it where that allows a bigger clock. On the square-screened Venu Sq 2 and the Venu X1 the layout is more compact, because the ring is a circle and everything has to stay inside it; Balanced, or the ring off, wins back that space.

6. Choosing a palette

PaletteBest for
Neon NightMaximum vibrancy: bright colours on true black. The default.
Max ContrastBlack, white and yellow only. The strongest choice for advanced low vision, and unaffected by any form of colour blindness.
Daylight BoldDark ink on white, for bright sun, and for eyes that see light text on a dark background smear — common with cataracts and astigmatism.
Amber NightWarm amber on black with no blue light. The least disruptive at night.

All value text is large and bold, and most colour pairs sit well above the 7:1 contrast ratio. Max Contrast reaches 21:1.

7. The progress ring

A thick band around the edge of the screen shows progress towards your step goal by default. It can instead show battery, your floors goal or your weekly active minutes, or be switched Off. It turns green the moment the goal is met. Goals are the ones already set on your watch and in Garmin Connect.

8. The alerts row

Beside the date, small icons appear only when something is true: an alarm is set, do not disturb is on, or the phone has disconnected. Unread messages are deliberately not an alert — with a phone paired that icon would be lit nearly all day. Use the Notifications field if you want a count. Turn the row off with Show alerts row.

9. Always-on and battery

  • With the wrist down, only the small seconds area is repainted each second. If your watch reports that this costs too much, the face gives up the seconds and keeps everything else. Turn Show seconds off for the lowest cost.
  • On AMOLED watches that need burn-in protection, the always-on view dims the colours, drops the ring and the fields, centres the clock, and shifts the whole face a few pixels each minute so no pixel holds one value for hours.

10. Watches

ClearSight runs on 69 watches on Connect IQ System 7. Each one was run in the simulator and its screen checked.

Left out after testing: the two-colour Instinct 3 Solar and Instinct E (40mm and 45mm). Their small round sub-window covers the battery field and the date collides with the top row; ClearSight has no layout for that screen. Watches older than Connect IQ System 7 — including fēnix 6, Forerunner 245 and 945, Venu, Venu Sq and vívoactive 4 — cannot run it.

11. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Customize is missingOlder watches without on-device settings show only Apply. Use Garmin Connect on your phone instead.
Garmin Connect will not open the settingsThe face was sideloaded. Use Customize on the watch, or install it from the store.
A field shows --That sensor is not on your watch, or there is no recorded history for it yet. Choose a different field.
The clock is smaller than I wantSwitch to Balanced or Giant clock, turn the ring off, or set unused fields to Off.
The seconds disappeared with the wrist downYour watch reported the per-second repaint as too expensive, so the face dropped it to stay on. Raise the wrist to see them.
Distance is in the wrong unitsIt follows the watch’s own units setting: Settings → System → Units on the watch.

Anything else: see Support, or write to info@vyteralabs.com with your watch model and software version.
