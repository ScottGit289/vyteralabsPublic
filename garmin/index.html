02

What they have in common

No phone required

Every one is a standalone Connect IQ project. There is no companion app to install and nothing that stops working when your phone is in a kit bag.

No account, no network

None of them signs you in, and none of them requests the Communications permission. Nothing you do is transmitted to Vytera Labs.

Settings that live on the watch

Every option can be set on the watch itself, and — for anything installed from the store — from Garmin Connect on the phone as well. Both edit the same values.

Honest numbers

A value that cannot be trusted is shown as a dash rather than guessed. Where a reading depends on a sensor your watch does not have, the app says so instead of inventing one.
