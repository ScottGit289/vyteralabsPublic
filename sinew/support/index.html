Sinew is a strength and cardio workout tracker that stays on your phone — for anyone who lifts or does cardio and wants a fast log with honest progress intelligence, from beginners to masters athletes. Everything runs on your device, with no account required. If something isn't working, or you have a question, we are happy to help.

What to include

Sinew collects no diagnostics and sends us nothing on its own, so a report is only as good as what you tell us:

  • Your device model and iOS version — Settings → General → About.
  • The Sinew version, from the app's own Settings screen.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it is something you can see.

If the app has crashed, Settings → Support in the app can email us a crash report. You see the email before it goes, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Please don't send us your backup export unless we ask.

It contains your whole training history. If a problem genuinely needs it we will say so, and explain what we will do with it and when we will delete it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Sinew has no accounts, no sign-up and no cloud. Open the app and start logging. Apple Health and the Oura Ring are optional extras, and the whole app works without them.

Is it free?

Yes. The free app is a complete tracker: unlimited workout and cardio logging, the full exercise library and custom exercises, templates, the rest timer, Apple Health read and write, exercise trends over the last eight weeks, this week's muscle balance, suggestions, the built-in coach, and backup export and import.

Sinew Pro is optional. It adds all-time history in trends, a 12-week muscle-balance view and a progression forecast, Oura Ring integration, readiness-adjusted suggestions, and the Apple Intelligence readout. It is £3.99 a month or £24.99 a year, each with a 7-day free trial, or £49.99 once for life. Prices are shown in the App Store and may differ by territory.

How is my progress calculated?

Each set is turned into an estimated one-rep max using the Epley formula — weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30) — so sets at different weights and reps can be compared. Sinew fits a trend line through an exercise's e1RM over time, alongside its top set and volume, and measures your latest session against that line. The result is an assessment of progressing, plateau or regressing, always with the number behind it. The user guide walks through it.

Why does an exercise say “plateau”?

Because its trend line has flattened: your recent sessions are landing on it, but it is not rising. That is a normal part of training, not a fault. The app suggests adding a rep to your working sets or changing the rep range. If it says regressing instead, the trend is falling, and it suggests checking your recovery and considering a deload. Early on, with only a session or two, the assessment has little to go on and will say so.

How do I change the weight increment, or switch between kg and lb?

In Settings. Choose kilograms or pounds (and kilometres or miles for cardio), and set the weight increment to 0.5, 1, 1.25, 2.5 or 5. Every weight stepper in the app moves by that amount from then on.

How do I connect Apple Health, and why is something missing?

Go to Settings → Apple Health, tap Connect, and tap Allow on Apple's permission sheet. Sinew reads workouts, body weight, resting heart rate, HRV and sleep entirely on your device, and writes completed workouts to Health only if you turn on Write workouts.

Apple grants permission per data type. If an import seems incomplete, open the Health app, go to Sharing → Apps → Sinew, and check that the type you expect is switched on. You can review or revoke access there, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health, at any time.

How do I connect Oura? (Pro)

With Sinew Pro, go to Settings → Wearables, turn on Enable wearable sync, tap Connect Oura, and sign in on Oura's own page. Sinew never sees your Oura password, and your health data comes straight to your phone. Your readiness, sleep and HRV then become a daily call — go heavy, normal, lighter or rest — on the Home tab. You can disconnect at any time.

What is “on-device intelligence”, and is my data safe?

It is an optional Sinew Pro setting — Settings → Intelligence, off by default — that uses Apple Intelligence to write your readout. It runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Foundation Models: nothing is sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising or model training.

It needs a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled. Otherwise Sinew uses its built-in coach instead, and the readout is labelled with which one wrote it. Turn it off at any time.

I have a new phone, or reinstalled. How do I get Pro back?

Go to Settings → Sinew Pro and tap Restore purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and verified on the device through Apple's StoreKit, so there is no Sinew account to sign in to. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Sinew and cancel there — or tap Manage subscription in the app, which takes you to the same place. A subscription renews unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple.

How do I back up or move my data?

Use Settings → Export backup to save a copy via the system share sheet — Files, AirDrop, email. On another device, use Import backup and choose that file.

Because your data lives only on your device, export a backup periodically. There is no cloud copy, and nobody else holds one for you.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Delete all data, or simply delete the app. Everything is stored on your device, so there is nothing held anywhere else to remove.

Is this medical advice?

No. Sinew is a fitness and information tool, not a medical device. Its assessments, suggestions and readiness calls are informational and general, and are no substitute for a qualified professional. See the terms.
