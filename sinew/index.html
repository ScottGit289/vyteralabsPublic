01

Super simple logging

Muscle group → exercise → sets. The picker groups a library of more than 150 exercises by Chest, Back, Shoulders, Biceps, Triceps, Forearms, Core, Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves and Full body, with search, your recent exercises, and custom ones for anything that is missing.

Each exercise shows what you did last time, greyed in. Tap Use last and the sets fill themselves; then nudge the weight and reps with steppers and tick each set done. A rest timer starts on its own after every set.

Your increment

The weight stepper moves by the plates you actually own: 0.5, 1, 1.25, 2.5 or 5.

Your unit

Kilograms or pounds for the bar, kilometres or miles for the road. Switch whenever you like.

Rest timer

Auto-starts after a set, for as many seconds as you choose. Or switch it off.

Templates

Start from a template such as “Push day”, or turn any past workout into one.

Finish, and you get a summary — volume, personal bests hit, estimated-1RM changes — before the workout is saved. The in-progress workout is persisted after every change, so a dropped phone or a dead battery loses nothing.

02

Progress you can trust

Every exercise is compared with its own trend line — estimated 1RM, top set and volume — rather than with a number pulled from thin air. The latest session is measured against where the trend says it should be, and the app says progressing, plateau or regressing, with the figure behind the verdict.

“Bench press is progressing: e1RM +4.2% over 6 weeks; last session 2.1% above trend.” A verdict is only useful if it shows its working.

Sinew reads all of your history, and adds a progression forecast.

03

Tells you what to train

Sinew counts your weekly hard sets per muscle group and sets them against an evidence-based 10–20 sets a week target band. Groups that are falling short are flagged, so you can see the gap before it becomes an imbalance.

Choose an area you want to improve and the app ranks exercises for it — the movement patterns you have trained least recently first, with variety of equipment, and a reason beside each one. With Sinew Pro the ranking is tuned to today's readiness.

04

Cardio is first-class

Runs, rides, rows, swims, walks and intervals, logged with duration, distance, average heart rate, RPE and notes. Weekly load in minutes and distance, a pace trend per activity, and your best efforts.

Workouts recorded by an Apple Watch or another app can be imported from Apple Health, de-duplicated, so nothing is counted twice.

05

On-device intelligence

A built-in coach assesses every trend and writes a plain readout, on every iPhone the app runs on.

On a compatible iPhone, Sinew can hand that narrative to Apple Intelligence, which writes it entirely on your device using Apple's Foundation Models — no network request, nothing sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else. It is off by default, clearly labelled when it is on, and the built-in coach steps in whenever it is unavailable.

06

Oura Ring, if you want it

With Sinew Pro, connect an Oura Ring and your readiness, sleep and HRV become one daily call before you train: go heavy, normal, lighter or rest. Sign-in uses Oura's own secure flow, so Sinew never sees your password, and your health data comes straight to your phone.

It is entirely optional. The whole app works without a wearable, and you can disconnect at any time.

The one exception, stated plainly: connecting Oura uses a small sign-in relay so the confidential app secret never ships inside the app. It brokers the sign-in exchange only, keeps nothing, and never receives your health data. The privacy policy covers it in full.

07

Private by design

Your data is stored on your device. There is no account, ever — nothing to sign up to and nothing to sign in to. There are no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no backend holding a copy of your training.

Data leaves your device only when you press Export backup, which hands a copy to the iOS share sheet for you to send wherever you choose. Full import too, and you can delete everything from Settings.

Sinew Pro

Free for 7 days from your first logged session, then Sinew Pro. There is no cut-down free tier: from the first launch every feature is open, with nothing to buy and no card to enter. The clock starts when you save your first workout or cardio session. Seven days later, Sinew Pro is needed to keep going. Your data never leaves the phone, and you can export it at any point, locked or not.
