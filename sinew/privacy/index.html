Sinew

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 11 October 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Sinew is built on a simple principle: your training data belongs to you and stays on your device.

The short version
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.
  • Everything you log, and every health metric you read from Apple Health or sync from a wearable, is stored on your device.
  • We operate no general backend and never receive a copy of your data.
  • Purchases of Sinew Pro are made with Apple. We receive no payment details and no identity.
  • The one piece of Sinew infrastructure that exists at all is a sign-in relay for Oura, described below. It never touches your health data.

What we collect

Nothing.

Sinew has no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We operate no general backend, and we never receive a copy of the workouts you log or the health metrics you read or sync.

The only times the app uses the network at all are when you choose to connect an Oura Ring (Sinew Pro), and when you buy, restore or check a purchase, which the app does with Apple. Your Oura health data is then fetched directly between your device and Oura. The single exception is the Oura sign-in step, relayed through a small Sinew-operated service so that Oura's confidential app secret never has to ship inside the app — explained under Oura Ring.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — workouts, sets, cardio sessions, custom exercises, templates, notes, settings — plus any health data you read from Apple Health or sync from a connected wearable, is stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage area, protected by the operating system's built-in data protection.

Wearable sign-in tokens are held in the device keychain and are never written to a backup export.

Apple Health (optional, off by default)

Sinew can connect to Apple Health, through Apple's HealthKit, entirely on your device — no sign-in, no relay, no network involved at all. It is optional and does nothing until you connect it in Settings → Apple Health and grant permission on Apple's sheet.

What it reads

If you allow it, Sinew reads workouts, body weight, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability and sleep. It uses them only for the app's own features on your device: importing workouts recorded by an Apple Watch or another app, and informing your trends and readiness.

What it writes

If you additionally turn on Write workouts, Sinew writes your completed workouts to Apple Health so that they appear alongside your other activity. This is opt-in and off until you enable it. Nothing else is written.

Health data read from Apple Health is never transmitted off your device, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with us or any third party. You can review or revoke access, per data type, at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Oura Ring (Sinew Pro, optional, off by default)

With Sinew Pro you can connect an Oura Ring so that readiness, sleep and heart-rate variability inform a daily training call. This is entirely optional and switched off until you enable it and connect.

  • Sign-in uses Oura's official OAuth page. Sinew never sees your Oura account password.
  • Your device fetches the health metrics you approved directly from Oura's servers. That data flows straight to your phone and is stored there — it never passes through, or is stored on, any Sinew service.
  • Completing Oura sign-in requires Oura's confidential app secret, which cannot safely live inside a phone app. So the sign-in hand-off — the step that turns your approval into an access token — is relayed through a small Sinew-operated service (running on Cloudflare at auth-sinew.scott-e9f.workers.dev and apitoken-sinew.scott-e9f.workers.dev) that holds that secret. It processes only the sign-in exchange; it keeps nothing, and it is not sent, and never sees, your Oura health data.
  • You choose the scope of data at Oura's consent screen, and you can disconnect at any time, which deletes the saved sign-in and all synced health data from your device.
  • Your use of the ring and Oura's own app remains governed by Oura's privacy policy.

On-device intelligence (Sinew Pro, optional, off by default)

Sinew can use Apple's on-device intelligence (Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models) to write your progress readout. It is off by default and only runs on a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled.

When you turn it on in Settings → Intelligence:

  • Your training and recovery data is processed entirely on your device by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to Apple's servers, to us, or to anyone else, and the app makes no network request to do it.
  • The data is never used to train any model, and never used for advertising.
  • If your device isn't compatible, or you leave the setting off, Sinew uses its built-in rules-based coach instead — the app works exactly the same either way, and the readout says which one wrote it.
  • You can turn it off at any time.

In-app purchases

Sinew Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple is the merchant. Apple handles the payment, your Apple ID, the subscription and any refund under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive no payment details and no identity: no card, no name, no email address.

Whether you hold Sinew Pro is checked on your device through Apple's StoreKit. There is no receipt server operated by us and no third-party purchase SDK in the app.

Data that leaves your device

Data leaves your device only when you explicitly export it using the Export backup button, which hands a copy to the system share sheet, or when you choose to email us a crash report from Settings → Support, which opens an email you see before sending. In both cases you choose the destination and you are responsible for wherever you send it.

Data deletion

You can delete all Sinew data at any time via Settings → Delete all data inside the app, or by deleting the app itself. Your workouts and synced health data live only on your phone, so there is no copy anywhere else to delete. The Oura sign-in relay described above stores none of your data. Workouts you chose to write to Apple Health remain in Health, under your control, until you delete them there.

Children

Sinew is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be listed in the App Store release notes of the version that introduces them, and the effective date above updated.
