Anchor · Privacy

Everything stays on your phone.

Anchor sends nothing anywhere. This is the whole policy, not the summary of one.

Last updated 4 September 2026 Vytera Labs Ltd
01

What we collect

Personal data collected None

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no email address, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK of any kind.

Vytera Labs cannot see your exercise record, your bladder diary, your notes, or even that you have installed the app.

02

What the app stores, and where

On your device only, inside the app’s own container and a shared App Group that only Anchor and its home-screen widget can read:

  • your exercise programme and settings
  • the sets you have completed, and any notes you added
  • your bladder diary entries
  • a small summary — sets today, target, streak — that the widget reads

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Settings › Erase everything deletes it without deleting the app.

03

Sharing, notifications and backups

Sharing

The only way data leaves your phone is if you tap Share summary and choose where to send it — an email to your physiotherapist, a note, a file. You choose the destination; Anchor has no destination of its own.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. They do not involve a server, and no push notification service is used.

Backups

If iCloud Backup is switched on for your device, Anchor’s data is included in that backup, in the same way as every other app. That backup is between you and Apple; Vytera Labs has no access to it.

Health data

Anchor does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Children

Anchor is intended for adults.

04

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete under the UK GDPR. If you want your data off your phone, use Erase everything, or delete the app.

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be a narrowing of an already empty set, not a widening.
