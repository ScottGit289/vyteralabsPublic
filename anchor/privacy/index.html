Anchor · Privacy

Everything stays on your phone.

Anchor sends nothing anywhere. This is the whole policy, not the summary of one.

Last updated 11 September 2026 Vytera Labs Ltd
01

What we collect

Personal data collected None

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no email address, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK of any kind. The only frameworks Anchor uses are Apple’s own, shipped with iOS.

Anchor makes no network requests of its own and we run no server for it. Vytera Labs cannot see your exercise record, your bladder diary, your notes, or even that you have installed the app.

02

What the app stores, and where

On your device only, inside the app’s own container and a shared App Group that only Anchor and its home-screen widget can read:

  • your exercise programme and settings
  • the sets you have completed, and any notes you added
  • your bladder diary entries
  • a small summary — sets today, target, streak — that the widget reads

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Settings › Erase everything deletes it without deleting the app. The one exception is described under the rating prompt below.

03

The rating prompt

Once you have completed a few sets, Anchor may ask iOS to show Apple’s standard “rate this app” sheet. That sheet belongs to Apple, not to us. If you choose to rate the app, the rating goes to the App Store exactly as it would if you left one there yourself. It does not pass through Anchor, and it carries nothing about your exercise record, your diary or your settings. We never learn whether you were shown the sheet, and we see only the same public ratings and reviews that any visitor to the App Store can see.

So that it cannot nag you, Anchor keeps three small values on your device: how many sets you have completed since it last asked, which version of the app last asked, and the date it last asked. Nothing else, and none of it leaves the phone. Those three values are the one thing Erase everything deliberately does not clear — wiping them would let the app start asking again immediately. Deleting the app removes them.

04

Sharing, notifications and backups

Sharing

The only way data leaves your phone is if you tap Share summary and choose where to send it — an email to your physiotherapist, a note, a file. You choose the destination; Anchor has no destination of its own.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. They do not involve a server, and no push notification service is used.

Backups

If iCloud Backup is switched on for your device, Anchor’s data is included in that backup, in the same way as every other app. That backup is between you and Apple; Vytera Labs has no access to it.

Health data

Anchor does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Children

Anchor is intended for adults.

05

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete under the UK GDPR. If you want your data off your phone, use Erase everything, or delete the app.

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be a narrowing of an already empty set, not a widening.
