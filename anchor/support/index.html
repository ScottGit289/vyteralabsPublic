Getting started

Which programme should I use?

If a physiotherapist or continence nurse has given you numbers, use theirs — Settings has a full plan editor. If not, answer the question on first launch about where you are in recovery and Anchor sets a conservative starting point.

Anchor says my programme is on hold

That happens when you have told it your catheter is still in. UK guidance after a prostatectomy is to start active pelvic floor exercises once the catheter has been removed. Tap My catheter has been removed when that happens. If your team has specifically told you to start sooner, there is an override on the same screen.

I cannot feel anything when I squeeze

Common, especially in the first weeks after surgery. It is the main reason to ask for a referral to a men's health physiotherapist, who can confirm you are working the right muscle. Anchor cannot check this for you.

Reminders

Reminders are not arriving

Open iOS Settings › Notifications › Anchor and make sure notifications are allowed. Anchor schedules reminders with iOS, so they arrive whether or not the app is open — but iOS will not deliver them if permission was declined.

I did my set at a different time. Does it count?

Yes. A set counts towards the day it was done, whatever time your reminder was set for.

Can I stop the reminder saying what it is for?

It already does. Discreet notifications are on by default, so the lock screen shows only Anchor — time for your set. You can turn the detail on in Settings if you prefer.

Your data

Where is my information stored?

On your iPhone only. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I give my record to my physiotherapist?

Progress › Share summary, or Settings › Export my data. That produces a plain-text summary plus CSV files of your sets and your bladder diary, and hands them to the iOS share sheet. You choose where they go.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

If you restore the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup, yes, in the same way as any other app's data. If you set the new phone up as new, no — export first.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Erase everything, or delete the app. Both remove all of it from the device permanently.

Appearance and accessibility

Anchor follows your iPhone's light or dark appearance, and can be pinned to either in Settings › Appearance. It supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion. Cues can be vibration-only, tones, spoken, or off entirely.

Reporting a problem

Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Anchor collects no diagnostics and sends us no crash reports, so your description is genuinely all we have to go on.

When to contact someone medical instead

Contact your clinical team or NHS 111 if you cannot pass urine, can only pass small amounts with pain, see blood you have not been told to expect, or develop burning, fever, new pain, or new weakness in the legs.

We cannot answer clinical questions. Anchor is not a medical device and we are not your clinicians.
