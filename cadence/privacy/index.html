Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 4 September 2026  ·  info@vyteralabs.com

Vytera Labs builds Cadence to be private by design. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version
  • Cadence collects nothing and sends nothing.
  • There is no account, no server, no analytics or tracking SDK, and no advertising.
  • Your habit data stays in one file on your iPhone. There is nothing for us to sell, share, or lose.

What we collect

Nothing. Not “nothing identifiable”, not “only anonymised usage data”. Nothing. The app contains no networking code, links no third-party frameworks, and has no mechanism capable of transmitting information anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

Your habits, your logged days, your notes and your settings are written to a single JSON file inside the app's own container on your device (group.com.vyteralabs.cadence). The app and its widgets read that same file.

The file never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it from Settings → Backup and restore and share it somewhere yourself.

Reading the Health app

If you switch it on for a specific habit, Cadence reads that one metric from the Health app — steps, active energy, exercise minutes, distance, water, mindful minutes, time asleep, the time you fell asleep, or workouts — so the habit completes itself.

  • Access is read-only. Cadence never writes to Health. The app declares a write purpose string only because Apple requires both to be present whenever the HealthKit entitlement exists; no write permission is ever requested.
  • It is opt-in per habit, and Cadence only ever asks for the metrics you have actually chosen.
  • The values are stored in Cadence's own local file on your device.
  • Nothing is transmitted, because the app has no networking code.
  • You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.
  • Decline the prompt, or never turn it on, and every other feature works unchanged.

Health data is read, on your device, at your instruction. It is not collected: it is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. That is why the App Store privacy label remains “Data Not Collected”.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled by iOS on your device. Cadence never registers for remote notifications, so no device token is ever created and there is no channel through which anything could be pushed to you.

Artificial intelligence

The weekly review is generated on your device.

On an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, Cadence uses Apple's Foundation Models framework, which runs the model locally. Prompts are not sent to Apple, to us, or to any third party. On every other device, Cadence uses its own analysis engine, written in Swift and running locally.

Either way, your habit names, notes and history never leave your iPhone.

Permissions

The app asks for two permissions, and only if you want them:

  • Notifications, so reminders can appear. Decline it and everything else works.
  • Health, read-only, and only if you connect a habit to a Health metric. Decline it and everything else works.

It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or background access.

Third parties

There are none. No SDKs, no libraries, no services, no trackers, no advertising.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Your data is yours, on your device:

  • Export it: Settings → Backup and restore → Create a backup.
  • Delete it: Settings → Erase all data, or delete the app.

Under UK GDPR and the EU GDPR, no personal data is processed by the developer, so no lawful basis, data protection officer or international transfer mechanism is engaged.

This website

These pages are static and carry no analytics, no cookies and no tracking of any kind. They load their typefaces from Google Fonts, in common with the rest of vyteralabs.com, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. That is a property of this website, not of the app: Cadence itself contacts nothing.

The trade-off, stated plainly

Because nobody else holds a copy of your data, nobody else can recover it for you. If you delete the app without a backup, your history is gone. Export a backup from Settings and keep it somewhere you trust.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version on this page. Any change that affected what the app does with your data would also be described in the release notes.
