Support

Something not working, or not behaving the way you expected? Email us and a person will read it.

info@vyteralabs.com

We aim to reply within two working days. Vytera Labs is UK-based, so replies arrive on UK hours.

What to include

Cadence collects no diagnostics and sends us nothing, so a bug report is only ever as good as what you tell us. The most useful message says:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version — Settings → General → About.
  • The Cadence version — Settings → About, at the bottom of the app.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it is something you can see.

Please don't send us your backup file unless we ask. It contains your entire habit history. If a problem genuinely needs it, we will say so and explain what we will do with it and when we will delete it.

Common problems

I connected a habit to Health and nothing happens

The likely cause is that Cadence was never granted read access. It asks when you save a habit with a metric on it — if you never saw that sheet, or dismissed it, go to iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Cadence and switch on the categories, then Settings → Health → Sync with Health now in Cadence.

Health answers an app it cannot read with an empty result rather than an error, which is why this can fail silently. After a sync, Settings → Health in Cadence tells you which of the two it is.

Also check that something actually writes the metric. Water, Mindful minutes and Asleep by only work if a watch, a ring or another app is recording them. Nothing can tell when your lights went out unless something was measuring.

My streak looks wrong

Open the habit and read the history grid. A faded square is a miss covered by a grace day; a grey square is a paused day. Both keep a streak alive on purpose.

If you tend to log after midnight, set Settings → Day rolls over at to 2am or later, so a late entry counts against the day you meant.

A day I completed shows as missed

Check the goal. A habit with a numeric target needs the full target — six of eight glasses is progress, not a completion. Tap the day in the heatmap to correct it.

How do I complete a habit that is 3× a week?

Log it on three separate days. The count on the right of the row — 2/3 this week — shows where you are.

Cadence counts days, not sessions: two gym sessions on the same Saturday count once, because streaks, grace days and the whole history are built on days. If you want to record volume rather than frequency, use a count goal, which stores a number per day.

How do I log a timed habit without sitting in the app?

Open the timer and use Already done it? underneath. It has quick jumps and a button that logs the full duration outright, so a 45-minute ride is one tap after the fact. You can also type exact minutes into any past day from the habit's history screen.

Reminders aren't arriving

Open Settings → Reminders in Cadence. If it says blocked, allow notifications for Cadence in iOS Settings, then use Rebuild all reminders.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Cadence never registers for push, so a Focus mode or Scheduled Summary can hold them back — check those too.

The Coach isn't telling me anything useful

In your first week it will not analyse trends, because there aren't any yet — it coaches your setup instead. Scores, weekday patterns and habit pairings start once there is a week of logged days behind you.

The Coach says Apple Intelligence isn't available

It needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with it switched on, and the model may still be downloading. Cadence works fully without it: you get the same analysis, written by the app instead of by the model.

A widget isn't updating

Widgets refresh when you log something and periodically after. Opening the app forces a refresh. If a widget shows nothing, check the habits are actually scheduled for today.

I deleted a habit by mistake

If you exported a backup, restore it and choose Merge. If not, it is gone — that is the honest cost of nobody else holding a copy of your data. Archiving, rather than deleting, keeps the whole history and just hides the habit.

Your data: export and delete

Everything lives in one file on your device. You do not need us to get it out, and you do not need us to destroy it.

You want toDo this
Export everything Settings → Backup and restore → Create a backup. Plain JSON, readable in any text editor.
Restore or merge a backup Settings → Backup and restore → Restore. Merge keeps what is already there.
Delete everything Settings → Erase all data, or delete the app. Nothing is left on any server, because it was never on one.

There is no account to close and no data of yours for us to hold, so there is nothing to request from us under GDPR. See the privacy policy for the reasoning.

Feature requests

Send them. Two things are worth knowing before you do, because they are design decisions rather than gaps:

  • There will be no cloud sync or web version. Both require a server, and the promise that nothing leaves your device is the product.
  • Grace days will never rewrite history. A forgiven day stays visible as missed and still counts against momentum. A tracker that flatters you is one you stop trusting.

Everything else is open to argument.
