What Vytera Labs does

Vytera Labs is a product company. It designs, builds and publishes its own software.

We work in two forms. SaaS platforms — subscription web software that businesses sign in to and rely on. And mobile apps — published to the App Store and Google Play for consumers and businesses alike.

Everything is owned outright. Vytera Labs develops its own intellectual property end to end, from first prototype to published product, and takes it to market directly — sold to the public and to businesses.

The studio is the software arm of Dellow Holdings, the parent company — the group's engine for building and shipping digital products.
