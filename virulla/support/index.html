Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

There's nothing to sign up for. On first launch, tap Create anonymous account, save the Recovery Key shown on the next screen, and tap Continue. That's it — no phone number or email.

What is the Recovery Key, and what if I lose it?

Because Virulla is anonymous, there is no "forgot password" and no way for us to identify you. The Recovery Key is the only way to restore your identity on a new device. Store it somewhere safe and private (a password manager is ideal). If you lose it and lose the device, the account cannot be recovered.

How do I start a chat?

Add a contact by scanning their QR code (Camera) or opening a contact link they share. Then open the chat and send text, a photo, or a video.

How do credits work?

Sending messages uses credits (text 1, photo 5, video 10). Every new account is granted 50 free credits so you can start right away. After that, credits are optional one-time in-app purchases — buy a pack when you need it. There's no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

Why do my chats look blank in a screenshot?

That's intentional. For privacy, message content is excluded from screenshots, screen recordings, and the app-switcher preview, so an open chat can appear blank in a capture. The app isn't broken — the content is fully visible on your screen.

How do I block or report someone?

In a chat, open the "•••" menu → Block user to stop them, or Report user to block them and send us the offending messages as evidence. Confirmed offenders are blocked at our relay so they can no longer use the service. See our Terms of Use.

How do I turn on dark mode?

Settings → Appearance → choose System, Light, or Dark. System follows your device setting.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete account & wipe device removes your identity and every message and key from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes its on-device data.

Is Virulla really secure?

Messages are end-to-end encrypted with a published, modern design (X3DH + Double Ratchet over Curve25519, AES-256-GCM), keys stay on your device, and our relay only ever sees ciphertext. No app can honestly promise to be "unhackable" — but there's no account linking messages to you, and no readable copy of your conversations on our servers. Keeping your device locked and your Recovery Key private matters too.
