1. What Virulla is

Virulla is a privacy-first messenger for people who want their conversations to stay private — really private.

Unlike most apps, Virulla asks for no phone number, no email, and no name. When you open it the first time, it creates an anonymous identity right on your phone. There is no central account for anyone to look up, leak, or hand over.

Every message, photo and video is end-to-end encrypted — scrambled on your device and only unscrambled on your contact's device. Not even Virulla's servers can read them. You can also set messages to disappear after a chosen time, after which they are permanently gone.

Virulla is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no ads. Sending messages uses credits, which you buy in small packs or receive as gift codes — more on that inside.

Anonymous

No phone, email or name. Your identity lives only on your device.

Encrypted end to end

Signal-grade cryptography. Only you and your contact can read messages.

Disappearing

Set a timer; when it ends the message is unrecoverable on both sides.

Photos & video

Share media that's encrypted before it ever leaves your phone.

Private groups

Group chats where an admin controls exactly who is in the room.

Extra protection

Face ID lock, screenshot blocking, contact verification and more.

2. Set up in a couple of minutes

There's no sign-up form. You create an anonymous identity and save one important key — that's it.

Install Virulla

Get Virulla from the App Store on your iPhone (iOS 17 or newer) and open it.

Create your anonymous account

Tap Create anonymous account. Virulla generates your private identity on the device. Nothing is sent to a server, and you're never asked for personal details.

Save your Recovery Key

You'll be shown a Recovery Key — a long secret code. This is the only way to restore your identity if you lose your phone. Copy it somewhere safe (a password manager is ideal), then tap Continue.

You're in — with 50 free credits

You'll land on the Chats screen, and your new account starts with 50 free credits so you can message straight away. A small status indicator shows Secure once you're connected. You're ready to add a contact.

Important — your Recovery Key is everything

Because Virulla is anonymous, there is no "forgot password" and no support team who can recover your account. If you lose your phone and your Recovery Key, your identity and messages cannot be restored. Save the key before you continue.

Why there's no phone number or email

Most messengers tie your account to a phone number, which links every message back to your real identity. Virulla deliberately doesn't. Your identity is a cryptographic key that lives on your device — which is exactly why the Recovery Key matters.

3. Connect with someone

You add people by exchanging a contact code — never a phone number. You only need to do it one way; the first message links you both.

Show your code

Open Add contact to display your own QR code and a text code, with a Share my code button.

Scan theirs

Tap Scan and point your camera at the other person's QR code.

Paste a code

If you can't scan, paste the text code they sent you and tap Add contact.

Send a link

Share your contact link by message or email; tapping it on an iPhone opens Virulla.

Your code contains no personal data

A contact code is just your anonymous address and public keys. Sharing it doesn't reveal your phone number, name, or location — it only lets someone start an encrypted conversation with you.

Verify it's really them (optional but recommended)

For sensitive conversations, open the chat menu and choose Verify safety number. Compare the number shown on both phones — in person or over a trusted channel. If they match, no one is secretly sitting in the middle of your conversation, and the contact gets a green shield. See section 7 for more.

4. Send messages & media

Open a chat and type as you would in any messenger. Text, photos and video are all encrypted on your device before they're sent.

What sending costs

Virulla uses credits so there's no subscription and no advertising. Each thing you send spends a small number of credits:

Your current balance is shown at the top of the chat list. If you run low, Virulla prompts you to top up — see section 4.

Photos & video

Tap the attachment button in a chat to pick a photo or video. It's encrypted on your device, and only your contact can open it. Videos play inside Virulla and are never saved to your phone in readable form.

Read receipts

When both people have read receipts enabled, you'll see a Read marker once your message has been opened. You can turn receipts off in Settings if you'd rather not share when you've read a message.

Tip

Sending fails with "not enough credits"? Tap the prompt to open the top-up screen, add a pack or redeem a gift code, and your message sends right away.

5. Buying & redeeming credits

Your account starts with 50 free credits. After that, credits are one-time purchases — buy what you need, when you need it. There's no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

Welcome credits

Every new account is loaded with 50 credits — enough for around 50 text messages — so you can start chatting before you buy anything.

Buy a credit pack

Open Credits from the chat list (the balance chip) and choose a pack. Payment is handled securely by Apple; Virulla never sees your card details.

Redeem a gift code

If you've been given a Virulla gift code, open Credits ▸ Redeem a gift code and either scan it or paste it in. Valid credits are added to your balance instantly. Each code can be redeemed once on your device.

About gift codes

Gift codes are issued by Vytera Labs and are cryptographically signed, so they can't be forged. If a code says it's invalid or already redeemed, check you copied all of it — or ask whoever gave it to you for a fresh one.

6. Messages that don't stick around

Set a timer on a conversation and messages delete themselves after the time you choose — on both your phone and your contact's.

Set a timer

In a chat, choose a disappearing-message duration. New messages in that chat will carry the timer.

The clock starts when it's read

An incoming message begins counting down once it's opened, so a short timer won't erase something before you've seen it.

Then it's gone

When the timer ends, the message is deleted and the key that could decrypt it is destroyed — so it can't be brought back.

"Unrecoverable" means unrecoverable

Disappearing messages are removed by throwing away their encryption key (crypto-shredding), not just hidden. Once gone, neither you, your contact, nor Virulla can restore them.

One honest limit

Virulla can hide message content from screenshots and screen recordings on the device, but it cannot stop someone from photographing their screen with a second camera. Disappearing messages protect what's stored, not what another person chooses to capture in the moment.

7. Private group chats

Groups work like one-to-one chats — end-to-end encrypted — with an admin who decides exactly who is in the room.

Create a group

Start a new group, give it a name, and pick contacts to add. You become the group admin.

Invite more people any time with an invite link or QR code from the group info screen — handy for adding someone who isn't a contact yet.

Everyone sends and receives normally; sending spends credits at the same rates as one-to-one chats.

Admin controls

Add & remove

Admins decide who joins and can remove members. Every change is cryptographically verified, so no one can sneak in.

Promote admins

Give another trusted member admin rights to help manage the group.

Invite links

Share a link or QR so new people create an account (if needed) and join automatically.

Leave any time

Anyone can leave a group; admins keep control of membership.

Membership can't be forged

Every add, remove or promotion is signed by an admin's key and checked by each member's app — there's no server deciding who's in the group, so membership can't be tampered with.

8. The features that protect you

Virulla layers several protections on top of end-to-end encryption. Here's what each one does and when to use it.

End-to-end encryption

Messages are encrypted on your device and decrypted only on your contact's, using X3DH, the Double Ratchet and AES-256 — the same approach trusted secure messengers use.

Verify a contact

Compare safety numbers to confirm no one is intercepting your chat. Verified contacts show a green shield.

Sealed sender

The server routes your message without learning who sent it — the sender's identity travels sealed inside the encryption.

Traffic-shape padding

Messages are padded to fixed sizes so the length of what you send can't be inferred from the network.

Face ID app lock

Require Face ID or Touch ID to open Virulla, so only you can read your chats. Turn it on in Settings.

PIN & duress PIN

Add a PIN, plus an optional duress PIN that silently wipes everything if you're forced to unlock the app.

Screenshot blocking

Message content is hidden from screenshots and screen recordings, and the app blanks itself in the app switcher.

Route over Tor

Optionally send your traffic through Tor/Orbot to hide your network location from the relay. Enable it in Settings.

Keyboard hardening

Third-party keyboards are blocked inside Virulla, so keystroke-logging keyboards can't see what you type.

Clipboard care

Copying your Recovery Key uses a local, auto-expiring clipboard that doesn't sync to your other devices.

An honest word on security

No software is unhackable, and Virulla doesn't claim to be. What it does give you is strong, well-understood protection for your message content and its deletion. The weakest link is usually a device itself — so keep your iPhone updated, locked, and in your control.

9. Alerts & quiet hours

Notifications are off by default and, when on, never reveal message content on your lock screen.

Private alerts

Turn on notifications in Settings to get a simple "New message" alert — never the sender or the text.

Quiet hours

Set a Do-Not-Disturb window (for example overnight) during which Virulla stays silent.

Quiet-hours allow list

Choose specific contacts who can still reach you during quiet hours — for the people who matter.

Tip

Because alerts never contain content, someone glancing at your lock screen learns only that a message arrived — not who from or what it said.

10. Restore, wipe & delete

You're always in control of your identity and your data — with the Recovery Key as the one thing only you hold.

Move to a new phone

Install Virulla on the new device and choose to restore with your Recovery Key. Enter the key you saved during setup and your identity comes back. Without the key, restoration isn't possible — by design.

Panic wipe

Virulla includes a fast wipe that clears your identity and messages from the device immediately. Use it if your phone is about to leave your control. If you've set a duress PIN, entering it at the lock screen triggers the same silent wipe.

Delete your account

You can delete everything from within the app at any time. Because Virulla stores your data on your device (not on a server tied to your name), deleting removes it at the source.

There's no undo

Wiping or deleting is permanent and cannot be reversed. Make sure you have your Recovery Key saved first if you intend to restore later.

11. Blocking & reporting

You decide who can reach you, and reporting abuse takes real action — even though Virulla is anonymous.

Block a contact

From a chat's ••• menu, block someone to stop receiving their messages. Unblock any time from Settings → Blocked users.

Report a user

From the ••• menu, tap Report user and pick a reason. Virulla automatically attaches the recent messages you received as evidence and blocks them for you — no copying needed.

What happens after you report

Your report goes to the Vytera Labs moderation team at abuse@virulla.com, which is monitored and responded to within 24 hours. Confirmed offenders can be blocked from the whole service — not just from you — and illegal content is reported to the authorities. Virulla has a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive behaviour.

Privacy and safety together

Being private doesn't mean being lawless. Because accounts are anonymous, we act on the abusive account rather than a person's identity — but that block is real and service-wide. Blocking, reporting and clear community rules keep Virulla a place people can trust.
