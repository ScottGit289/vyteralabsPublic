User Guide

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  For: Virulla on iPhone  ·  support@virulla.com

Virulla is an anonymous, end-to-end encrypted messenger. There is no phone number, no email and no account — just an identity created on your device. This guide covers setting it up, the one thing you must not lose, and how the privacy features behave so they do not surprise you.

Read this first
  • Your Recovery Key is the only way to restore your identity. We cannot reset it, because we do not know who you are.
  • Chats look blank in screenshots and in the app switcher. That is deliberate, not a fault.
  • Sending messages uses credits. Every new account starts with 50 free.
  • Virulla is rated 17+ and has zero tolerance for abusive behaviour. Reports are actioned within 24 hours.

1. Creating your identity

There is nothing to sign up for. On first launch, tap Create anonymous account, save the Recovery Key shown on the next screen, and tap Continue.

No phone number, email or name is ever asked for or stored. The identity is generated on your device, and the keys stay there.

2. The Recovery Key

This is the single most important screen in the app, so it is worth being blunt about it.

Because Virulla is anonymous, there is no "forgot password" and no way for us to identify you. The Recovery Key is the only way to restore your identity on a new device.

Store it somewhere safe and private — a password manager is ideal. If you lose it and lose the device, the account cannot be recovered. That is not a policy we can make an exception to; it is a consequence of us holding nothing about you.

3. Adding a contact

Add a contact by scanning their QR code with the camera, or by opening a contact link they share with you.

Because there are no phone numbers and no address book on our side, this exchange is how two people find each other. There is no directory to search.

4. Sending messages

Open the chat and send text, a photo, or a video. Media is shared in 1:1 chats and groups, and is encrypted exactly the same way as text.

5. How credits work

Sending a message uses credits:

  • Text — 1 credit
  • Photo — 5 credits
  • Video — 10 credits

Every new account is granted 50 free credits so you can start straight away. After that, credits are optional one-time in-app purchases — buy a pack when you need one.

There is no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

6. Disappearing messages

Set messages to self-destruct after they are read, or after a timer, so conversations do not linger on either device.

7. Why your chats look blank in a screenshot

Message content is deliberately excluded from screenshots, screen recordings and the app-switcher preview. An open chat can therefore appear blank in a capture.

The app is not broken — the content is fully visible on your screen. The point is that a glance over your shoulder, or a capture, does not spill a conversation.

Worth being realistic about the limit: this protects against the device's own capture paths. It cannot stop someone photographing your screen with another camera.

8. Blocking and reporting

In a chat, open the ••• menu:

  • Block user stops them contacting you.
  • Report user blocks them and sends us the offending messages as evidence.

Confirmed offenders are blocked at our relay so they can no longer use the service. Abuse reports are actioned within 24 hours, and can also be sent to abuse@virulla.com.

Every user agrees to the Terms of Use before messaging.

9. Appearance

Settings → Appearance — choose System, Light or Dark. System follows your device setting.

10. How the encryption works

Virulla uses a published, modern encryption design: X3DH key agreement and the Double Ratchet, over Curve25519, with AES-256-GCM — the same family of techniques used by other serious private messengers.

Keys live only on your devices. Messages and media are encrypted on your device and decrypted only on your contact's.

The server is a zero-knowledge relay: it routes encrypted envelopes between devices and deletes them on delivery. It holds no identities, no address books and no keys, so there is nothing on it to read, sell, or hand over.

Honest about security

No app can promise to be "unhackable", and we do not. What we can say plainly is how Virulla is built: end-to-end encryption with keys on your device, a server that only ever sees ciphertext, and no account tying messages to a real-world you.

Strong encryption reduces risk; it does not remove the need for good habits. Keep your device locked and your Recovery Key private.

11. Deleting everything

Settings → Delete account & wipe device removes your identity and every message and key from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes its on-device data.

Because nothing about you is stored server-side, that removes everything. There is no account left behind for us to close.

12. Getting help

Email support@virulla.com — we aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

To report abuse, use the in-app Report option in the chat ••• menu, or email abuse@virulla.com. Abuse reports are actioned within 24 hours.
