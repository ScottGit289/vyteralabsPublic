01

What you get

Anonymous identity

On first launch the app creates an identity on your device and shows you a Recovery Key. No phone number, email, or name is ever asked for or stored.

End-to-end encryption

Messages and media are encrypted on your device and decrypted only on your contact's. Our relay handles ciphertext it can't read.

Disappearing messages

Set messages to self-destruct after they're read or after a timer, so conversations don't linger.

Text, photos & video

Share media in 1:1 chats and groups, encrypted the same way as text.

Screenshot resistance

Message content is kept out of screenshots and the app-switcher preview, so a glance or a capture doesn't spill a conversation.

Block & report

Block anyone in a tap, and report abuse with the offending messages attached as evidence. Confirmed offenders are cut off at our relay.

02

How it works

Virulla uses a published, modern encryption design — X3DH key agreement and the Double Ratchet, over Curve25519, with AES-256-GCM — the same family of techniques used by other serious private messengers. Keys live only on your devices. The server is a zero-knowledge relay: it routes encrypted envelopes between devices and deletes them on delivery. It holds no identities, no address books, and no keys, so there is nothing on it to read, sell, or hand over.

Honest about security. No app can promise to be "unhackable," and we don't. What we can say plainly is how Virulla is built: end-to-end encryption with keys on your device, a server that only ever sees ciphertext, and no account tying messages to a real-world you. Strong encryption reduces risk; it doesn't remove the need for good habits like keeping your device locked and your Recovery Key private.
03

Private by design

Nothing collected

No accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising or tracking SDKs. Our Privacy Manifest declares "Data Not Collected".

You hold the keys

Your identity and message keys are generated and stored on your device. Your Recovery Key is the only way to restore an account — we can't reset it for you.

Erase in a tap

Delete your account and wipe every message and key from the device from Settings. Because nothing about you is stored server-side, that removes everything.

Community standards. Virulla has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour. Every user agrees to our Terms of Use before messaging, and reports are actioned within 24 hours. Rated 17+.
