Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vytera Labs Ltd  ·  Effective: 5 September 2026  ·  privacy@virulla.com

Vytera Labs builds Virulla to be anonymous and private by design. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version
  • There is no account — no phone number, email, or name. Your identity is created and stored on your device.
  • Messages and media are end-to-end encrypted. Our relay only ever handles ciphertext and deletes it on delivery.
  • We keep no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. Our Privacy Manifest declares "Data Not Collected".

No account, no identifiers

Virulla does not ask for a phone number, email address, or name. On first launch the app generates an anonymous cryptographic identity on your device and shows you a Recovery Key — the only way to restore that identity. We never see your Recovery Key, and we cannot reset it.

Your messages

Text, photos, and video you send are end-to-end encrypted on your device and can be decrypted only on your recipient's device. To move an encrypted envelope between devices, the app connects to a Vytera Labs relay server. The relay:

  • routes ciphertext only — it cannot read your messages or media;
  • holds no user identities, contact lists, or encryption keys;
  • deletes a message once it has been delivered; undelivered messages are held briefly, encrypted, then expire.

Because the relay is "zero-knowledge", there is no readable store of your conversations for us to search, sell, or disclose.

Device permissions

  • Camera — used only when you scan a contact's QR code or capture media to send. We do not access the camera in the background.
  • Photo Library — used only to attach media that you pick.
  • No location, contacts, microphone-in-background, or health access is requested.

What we collect: nothing

Vytera Labs does not collect, receive, or store personal data about you on any server. Specifically:

  • No accounts / sign-in to Vytera Labs, and no third-party or social login.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services that send data off your device.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. This matches the app's bundled Privacy Manifest ("Data Not Collected").

In-app purchases

Messaging uses credits. The app is free and credits are optional consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms. Vytera Labs receives the purchase confirmation needed to grant credits on your device; we do not receive your payment card or Apple account details.

Abuse reports

If you report a user, the app blocks them and opens a pre-filled email to our moderation address (abuse@virulla.com) that includes the recent messages you received from that user, as evidence. You choose whether to send it. We use the information solely to review the report and, where warranted, block the offending address at our relay. See the Terms of Use.

Group invites & links

Group and contact invites can be shared as links or QR codes. Opening one may create an anonymous identity (if you don't have one yet) and then join the group or add the contact. No personal data is involved in a link.

Age

Virulla is rated 17+ and is intended for adults. It is not directed to children and is not a "Made for Kids" app.

Your controls

  • Delete account & wipe device: Settings → "Delete account & wipe device" removes your identity and every message and key from the device. Because nothing tied to you is stored server-side, this deletes everything.
  • Block or report any user from the chat menu.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its on-device data.

Security

Messages use a published end-to-end encryption design (X3DH + Double Ratchet over Curve25519, with AES-256-GCM); keys are stored in your device's secure storage. No app can promise to be "unhackable", and we don't. What we can state plainly is that we hold no readable copy of your messages and no account linking them to a real-world identity, which removes the server-side breach surface for your conversations. Keeping your device locked and your Recovery Key private remains important.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version on this page.
